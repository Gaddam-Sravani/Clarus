Rules and Regulations:
1) The duration of short film should not exceed 15 minutes.
2) The short film can be any genre i.e., you can submit your work that includes
elements on social awareness, action, horror, love and comedy.
3) The short film must be shot in campus premises only. Students should not
disturb other classes and should not miss their academic schedules.
4) The judge will disqualify or reject a film if they have reason that it does not
comply with entry terms and conditions thoroughly.
5) Photos submitted for photography, selfie challenge, groupie challenge must be
in JPEG/JPG/PNG format without any editing, that too pictures that are taken
inside campus.
6) Vines, spoofs and Mannequins should not exceed 4 minutes, Dubsmash and
Tiktoks should not exceed 2 minutes. It is mandatory.
7) The last date of submission will be on 27th January, 2020.
