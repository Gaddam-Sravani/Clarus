RGUKT BASAR — STUDENT COUNSELING CENTER (SCC)
================================================================================

OVERVIEW & MANDATE
The Student Counseling Center (SCC) at RGUKT Basar provides students with comprehensive Guidance and Counseling across Personal, Educational, and Career domains to foster healthy personality development, emotional resilience, and sound mental health.

SERVICES OFFERED
1. 24×7 Counselor Availability: Qualified professional counselors accessible on all days for student assistance and emergency support.
2. Gender-Specific Cells: Separate counseling cells for boys and girls designed for confidential and gender-specific requirements.
3. Psychological Assessment Laboratory: Dedicated facility equipped for scientific psychological testing and clinical evaluations.
4. Dual Center Locations: Counseling facilities situated both within the core Academic Area and at the Campus Hospital.
5. Motivational & Development Programs: Periodic institutional workshops on personality growth, emotional well-being, and stress management.
6. Eminent Guest Interactions: Invited lectures and interactive sessions with distinguished personalities to promote social inclusion without inhibition.
7. Psychiatrist Visits: Specialized psychiatric consultations and clinical visits arranged on demand.
