About the Student Counseling Center
The Student Counseling Center (SCC) helps the students of RGUKT-Basar with necessary Guidance and Counseling — whether Personal, Educational, or Career-oriented — for good personality development and sound mental health.
Services Offered
Availability of qualified counsellors on all days (24×7)
Separate cells for boys and girls with gender-specific requirements
Laboratory for psychological assessments
Central counseling centre in the academic area and at the hospital
Periodic programmes for motivational and personality development
Invited talks and interaction with eminent personalities for social inclusion without inhibition
Psychiatrist's visits on demand
