1. PANORAMA — CULTURAL & SOCIAL ACTIVITIES CLUB (TRINAYANA)
Organized by: Cultural and Social Activities Club / Team TRINAYANA
Official Notice: Notice-53 | Date: 7th January, 2020
Tagline: "PANORAMA — A wide view"

Competition Categories (8 Events):
1. Short Films
2. Vines
3. Spoofs
4. Dubsmash / TikTok
5. Photography
6. Selfie Challenge
7. Groupie Challenge
8. Mannequin Challenge

Rules and Regulations:
- Short Films: Duration must not exceed 15 minutes. Allowed genres include social awareness, action, horror, love, and comedy. Must be shot strictly on campus premises without disturbing classes or missing academic schedules.
- Vines, Spoofs & Mannequins: Duration must not exceed four (4) minutes.
- Dubsmash & TikToks: Duration must not exceed two (2) minutes.
- Photography, Selfie & Groupie Challenges: Photos must be in JPEG, JPG, or PNG format without any digital editing and must be captured inside the campus premises.
- Submission & Disqualification: Submissions failing to comply with entry terms will be rejected by the judges. Last date of submission was 27th January, 2020.

2. LIT FEST — DEPARTMENT OF ENGLISH (HUMANITIES)
Organized by: Department of English, RGUKT Basar | Event: LIT FEST 2K16 | Date: 15/02/2016

Events & Competition Rules (8 Events):
1. Literary Quiz: On-site competition; team consists of three (3) members.
2. Calligraphy: On-site competition. Writing sample provided at contest; participants given 15 to 20 minutes to complete. Participants must bring their own writing tools (fountain pen, ink pen, pencil).
3. Word Wizard: On-site competition with individual participation. Involves solving puzzles, crosswords, etc. Time announced on spot.
4. Cartoon Scope: Individual participation. Topics announced one day prior to competition.
5. Extempore (Public Speaking):
   - Objectives: To develop spontaneity (thinking on the spot and expressing thoughts) and strengthen oral interactive speaking skills.
   - Format: 3 minutes preparation time; 2 minutes speaking time on current affairs topics. Warning bell given at 1.5 minutes (1 minute 30 seconds).
6. Spell Bee: On-site competition with individual participation.
7. Dumb Charades: Team consists of three (3) members. Acting participant cannot speak or point to objects/persons; only one split allowed per word.
8. Skit: Inter-group theatrical performance as per registered candidate guidelines.
