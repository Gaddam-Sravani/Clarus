RULES OF REVALUATION

1. Revaluation application will be accepted upto 2 weeks from the date of publication of results.


2. No action will be taken unless this is accompanied with a Photostat copy of the memorandum of marks or Grade Card.


3. The prescribed fee for revaluation is as per revaluation notification and shall be paid through challan at SBI, RGUKT, Basar branch. In no case the fee is refundable.


4. The result will not be communicated to the individual candidate personally. However, they may know the results through Hub or from the Examination Branch directly. The memos of the candidate whose results have changed due to revaluation will be issued after declaration of revaluation results. In other cases no information will be given.


5. If the difference between the original marks and the revaluated marks is 10% or more of the maximum marks of the paper, the revalued marks shall be taken into consideration, and the average of original marks and revalued marks will be awarded to the candidate for the declaration of final result of the revaluation.
