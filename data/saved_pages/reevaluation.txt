RULES AND REGULATIONS FOR REVALUATION — RGUKT BASAR
Authority: Office of the Controller of Examinations (CoE) & Director, RGUKT Basar. Notification Date: 02/07/2016.

1. APPLICATION PROCEDURE & DEADLINE
- Submission Window: Revaluation applications are accepted up to two (2) weeks (14 days) from the date of publication of results.
- Mandatory Enclosure: Applications must be accompanied by a photostat copy of the memorandum of marks or official Grade Card. No action will be taken without this document.
- Application Format: Submitted in writing to the Director / Controller of Examinations.

2. PRESCRIBED REVALUATION FEE & PAYMENT METHOD
- Fee: As prescribed per the university examination notification (Rs. 100/- per subject).
- Payment Mode: Must be paid through bank challan at State Bank of India (SBI), RGUKT Basar branch.
- Refund Policy: Under no circumstances is the revaluation fee refundable.

3. RESULT ANNOUNCEMENT & MARKS MEMO ISSUANCE
- Notification Channel: Revaluation results are NOT communicated to individual candidates personally. Students can check their results online through the Hub (hub.rgukt.ac.in) or directly at the Examination Branch.
- Revised Memos: Updated marks memorandums are issued only to candidates whose results/grades have changed after revaluation. In cases where marks do not change, no separate notice is issued.

4. 10% DIFFERENCE THRESHOLD & FINAL SCORE FORMULA
- Eligibility for Mark Change: A revaluation score is taken into consideration ONLY IF the difference between the original marks and the revaluated marks is 10% or more of the maximum marks of the paper.
- Final Marks Awarded: If the difference meets or exceeds the 10% threshold, the final marks awarded to the candidate is calculated as:
  Final Marks = (Original Marks + Revalued Marks) / 2
  *(The candidate is awarded the arithmetic average of the original marks and revalued marks).*
- If the difference is less than 10%, the original marks remain unchanged.
