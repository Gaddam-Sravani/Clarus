About NIRF
The National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF) was approved by the Ministry of Education (then MHRD) in 2015. It outlines a methodology to rank institutions across the country based on five broad parameters: Teaching, Learning & Resources (TLR); Research and Professional Practice (RP); Graduation Outcomes (GO); Outreach and Inclusivity (OI); and Peer Perception (PR).
RGUKT Basar participates in the NIRF ranking process annually and submits data under the
University
category.
