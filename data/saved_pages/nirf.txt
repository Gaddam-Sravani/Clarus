2. NIRF CORE EVALUATION PARAMETERS (5 BROAD METRICS)
Institutions are ranked nationally based on five broad parameters:
1. Teaching, Learning & Resources (TLR): Faculty-student ratio, faculty qualifications, financial resources, and utilization.
2. Research and Professional Practice (RP): Publications, patents, sponsored research, and consultancy projects.
3. Graduation Outcomes (GO): Ph.D student output, campus placement rates, and student examination performance.
4. Outreach and Inclusivity (OI): Regional diversity, gender diversity, and economic/social diversity.
5. Peer Perception (PR): Academic peer perception and corporate employer feedback.
