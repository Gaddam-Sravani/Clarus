TRAINING & PLACEMENT CELL (T&P CELL) — RGUKT BASAR
Location: Training & Placement Cell, RGUKT Basar, Basara, Nirmal – 504107, Telangana.

1. T&P CELL OVERVIEW & SCOPE
The Training & Placement Cell at RGUKT Basar acts as a catalyst in providing students with options to develop themselves professionally into streams matching their aptitudes.
- Core Responsibilities: Organizes campus recruitment drives, conducts mock interviews, group discussions, pre-placement talks, and interactive sessions with industry experts.
- Personality Development: Covers communication, presentation skills, group dynamics, and career planning.
- Committee Structure: Includes departmental faculty representatives and student committees working together to build corporate relations.

3. MANDATORY SUMMER INTERNSHIP RULES (E3 STUDENTS)
- Eligibility & Timing: All third-year (E3) B.Tech students are required to complete an eight-week (8-week) summer internship commencing immediately after E3 Semester 2 (E3 S2) examinations, as mandated by the RGUKT curriculum.
- Nature of Work: Internships must be of a problem-solving nature; generic training courses do not qualify.
- Eligible Host Organizations: Government agencies, CSIR laboratories, IITs, IIMs, DRDO, public sector enterprises, and leading IT companies.
- In-House Restriction: In-house (RGUKT campus) internships are strictly not permitted.

4. TRAINING PROGRAMMES OFFERED
- Aptitude Training: Quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, and verbal ability for competitive exams and placement recruitment tests.
- Soft Skills: Communication, presentation skills, group discussion (GD), group dynamics, and personality development workshops.
- Technical Skills: Core engineering concepts, data structures, algorithms, and domain-specific technical training.
- Mock Interviews: Simulated HR and technical interviews with expert feedback to prepare students for real campus drives.
- Industry Interaction: Pre-placement talks, guest lectures by industry experts, alumni interactive sessions, and industrial visits.
- Certification Courses: NPTEL, Coursera, and vendor-specific certifications to strengthen student profiles.
