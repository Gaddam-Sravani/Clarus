About the Department
Established 2010
The Department of Physics at RGUKT Basar provides foundational physics education for all B.Tech engineering programmes. The department offers a Minor in Physics as an elective programme for students across all engineering disciplines. It provides rigorous theoretical and experimental training in classical and modern physics, equipping students with strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
Vision & Mission
Vision
To impart quality education in physics, nurture scientific temperament among students, and promote research in frontier areas of physics.
Mission
To provide strong fundamentals in physics for all engineering students. To encourage research, innovation, and scientific inquiry. To develop students' problem-solving and analytical skills through rigorous physics education.
