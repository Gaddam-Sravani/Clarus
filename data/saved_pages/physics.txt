DEPARTMENT OF PHYSICS — RGUKT BASAR

1. DEPARTMENT OVERVIEW & PROGRAMMES
- Established: 2010
- Total Faculty Members: 29
- Academic Scope: Imparts foundational physics theoretical and experimental training for all B.Tech engineering programmes and Pre-University Course (PUC).
- Specialized Elective Programme: Offers an official "Minor in Physics" as an elective programme for students across all engineering disciplines.

2. VISION & MISSION
- Vision: To impart quality education in physics, nurture scientific temperament among students, and promote research in frontier areas of physics.
- Mission:
  * To provide strong fundamentals in physics for all engineering students.
  * To encourage research, innovation, and scientific inquiry.
  * To develop students' problem-solving and analytical skills through rigorous physics education.
