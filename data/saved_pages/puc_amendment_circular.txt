OFFICIAL ACADEMIC CIRCULAR: AMENDMENT IN PUC COMPLETION RULES (B19 BATCH ONWARDS)
Authority: Academic Section, RGUKT Basar | Date: July 16, 2021
Reference: Approval of Minutes of XXVII Executive Committee (EC) Meeting held on 13.01.2021
Circular No: RGUKT/Acad/2021/NB-Hub/019
Contact: +91-94923 02117 | academics@rgukt.ac.in

1. ALTERATION OF CLAUSE X(1)(3)(e) — COMPLETION OF PUC COURSE
Subject: Amendment regarding students who fail to meet the promotion criteria to B.Tech 1st Year (as mentioned in X(1)(3)(d)), but have satisfied the PUC pass requirements.

2. RESOLUTION OF THE EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE (XXVII EC):
"The Executive Committee (EC) has reviewed the PUC program and resolved to permit PUC students with backlogs to write end semester examinations along with regular students beyond three (3) years without providing accommodation and food.

EC has accorded to implement this from B19 batch students onwards, and such students shall be awarded a PUC Completion Certificate upon clearing all backlog subjects.

CRITICAL CONDITIONS:
1. Loss of Engineering Eligibility: All such students who clear PUC beyond three (3) years automatically LOSE their eligibility for B.Tech Engineering admission at RGUKT Basar.
2. No Exemption: The Executive Committee has strictly resolved to give NO EXEMPTION on the requirement of completing the 2-year PUC program within three (3) years to be eligible for the 4-Year B.Tech engineering program at RGUKT."

3. SIGNATORIES & CIRCULATION
- Issued by: Associate Dean of Science & Humanities and Associate Dean of Engineering, RGUKT Basar.
- Circulated to: PS to Vice Chancellor, Administrative Officer, Deans, HoDs, Controller of Examinations (CoE), PRO, Faculty, and Student Hub.
