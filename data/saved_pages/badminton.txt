Rules and Regulations of the Badminton Arena

1. The badminton arena will be open on all working days in both the sessions. The timings of the sessions are 5:30 am 7:30 am and 5:30 pm - 8:00 pm for both boy students and girl students.


2. Proper sports attire (Shorts or track pants, sports t-shirt or sleeveless, and badminton non-marking shoes) is a must to play on the wooden courts.


3. Don't drop sweat on the wooden floor.


4. Spilling water on the wooden floor is strictly prohibited.


5. Always carry a dry towel or a handkerchief to wipe your sweat.


6. Do not bring water bottles near to the court as it could spill water on the court. Keep the water bottles outside the badminton arena.


7. Bing your own racquets and shuttles when you come to play.


8. Do not practice in between the courts. Do not disturb other players when they play.


9. One court is reserved for girl students, two courts are reserved for boy students one for learners and one for professionals and one court is reserved for the staff.


10. If the courtsreserved for a particular group is empty anyone can play but should vacate when they come.


11. If a court is crowded with players, losers out will be followed and no winning team can play more than three matches of one set each.


12. The courts can be occupied in first come first serve basis and the same rotation should be followed.


13. One should warm up one's body before play and cool it down after the play. Warming up and cooling down must not be done on the badminton courts.


14. Do not use mobile phones or play music in badminton arena. Keep your mobiles in silent mode or place them near the gallery when you come to the badminton arena.


15. When branch games are conductedonly one person from each branch playing is allowed to be near the court to give suggestions and to call time outs. The rest of the members must sit in the gallery and meet the players only during the timeouts and breaks only. If a team fails to follow this the entire team will be disqualified.


16. During branch games and staff leagues referee's decision will be final. If any team argues with the referee or the line umpires then the entire team will be disqualified.


17. If anyone wants to conduct a badminton tournament, prior permission of the sports convener must be sought.


18. Sports convener reserves the right to add, modify or delete any rules depending on the circumstances.


19. You are responsible for your own belongings.


20. Please make sure that the lights are switched off if you are the last to leave the arena.


21. Do not litter and keep the badminton arena clean.


22. Do not involve in unnecessary activities in the badminton arena.


23. Those who do not follow the etiquettes of the badminton arena are liable for strict punishment.
