VIII. AWARD OF DEGREE & UNIVERSITY GOLD MEDALS — RGUKT BASAR

1. AWARD OF UNDERGRADUATE DEGREES
- 6-Year Integrated Structure: Selected candidates after 10th class enter the 6-year integrated undergraduate engineering programme combining a 2-Year Pre-University Course (PUC) and a 4-Year Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech).
- PUC Degree Award: Students are officially awarded a PUC Certificate/Degree upon successful completion of the 2-Year PUC programme (all 4 semesters).
- B.Tech Degree Award: Students are officially awarded the Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) degree upon successful completion of the 4-Year B.Tech programme across their allocated engineering discipline.

2. AWARD OF UNIVERSITY GOLD MEDALS (CRITERIA & ELIGIBILITY)
i. University Gold Medal (Overall Topper):
   - Eligibility: Awarded to the student securing the highest Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) across the entire B.Tech programme in a single attempt.
ii. Branch-Wise Gold Medal (Department Toppers):
   - Eligibility: Awarded to the student securing the highest CGPA in the B.Tech programme in a single attempt within their respective Engineering Discipline (CSE, ECE, EEE, ME, CE, CHEM, MME).
