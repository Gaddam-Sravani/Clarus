VISION AND MISSION — RGUKT BASAR

1. INSTITUTIONAL VISION
The vision of the University is to provide quality engineering education to meritorious rural youth deprived of this opportunity, through an innovative blend of modern computer-assisted, learner-centric instructional methodology along with rigorous traditional teaching in a world-class ambiance.

Driving Causes for the Vision:
- Most talented rural youth wishing to pursue higher education have no access to renowned and world-class institutions and facilities.
- They come from economically impoverished backgrounds that severely inhibit their choices and passions.

Key Envisaged Outcomes for Students:
- To experience educational standards in engineering education that are of world-class quality.
- To enroll in a top technical university that provides free/subsidized education to the deserving 1% of the creamy layer of students who have passed out of the 10th (X) class.


2. INSTITUTIONAL MISSION
The mission of RGUKT Basar is defined through the following four core objectives:
1. To create a Knowledge Hub for producing qualified manpower possessing Post Graduate and Doctoral qualifications in different branches of Engineering.
2. To develop the campus as a highly research-centric institute.
3. To promote and propagate innovative teaching and research programs and create specialized centers of Learning/Training.
4. To help the students launch on a path of self-discovery.
