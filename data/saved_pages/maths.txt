About the Department
Established 2010
The Department of Mathematics strives for excellence among Indian and international academic institutions, offering comprehensive curricula at undergraduate and postgraduate levels. The department employs 19 faculty members across diverse mathematical areas and regularly hosts distinguished mathematicians. Faculty support students preparing for competitive exams including GATE, GRE, and CAT.
Vision & Mission
Vision
To be among the best mathematics departments in the country and to establish an international reputation as a centre for research and teaching in mathematics.
Mission
Transforming young people into competent professionals with strong theoretical and practical knowledge. Producing graduate students with robust foundations for research or industry careers. Cultivating mathematical interests and motivating research in mathematical sciences. Contributing to national development, particularly in the Telangana region, through research and training.
