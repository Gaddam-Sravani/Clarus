RGUKT BASAR — DEPARTMENT OF TELUGU
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
The Department of Telugu offers core language, literature, communication, and cultural coursework for students in the Pre-University Course (PUC) and undergraduate programs, nurturing native literary proficiency and cultural heritage.
