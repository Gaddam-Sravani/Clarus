About the Department
Established 2010
The Department of Humanities and Social Sciences at RGUKT Basar comprises the Department of English and the Department of Telugu. The department provides foundation courses in English communication, Telugu language, and humanities subjects for all engineering students, fostering holistic development alongside technical education.
