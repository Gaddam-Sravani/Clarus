RGUKT BASAR — DEPARTMENT OF HUMANITIES AND SOCIAL SCIENCES (H&SS)
================================================================================

OVERVIEW & ESTABLISHMENT
- Established: 2010
- Total Faculty Members: 28
- Organizational Structure: Comprises the Department of English and the Department of Telugu.

ACADEMIC MANDATE
The Department of Humanities and Social Sciences provides foundational and advanced courses in English communication, Telugu language, literature, and social sciences for all Pre-University Course (PUC) and B.Tech engineering students. Its curriculum is designed to foster holistic personal development, analytical thinking, and ethical understanding alongside rigorous technical education.

CONSTITUENT DEPARTMENTS & SCOPE

1. Department of English
   - Scope: Develops communication excellence through interactive language laboratories, professional soft-skills training, and standardized competitive examination preparation, including TOEFL, IELTS, and GRE.

2. Department of Telugu
   - Scope: Preserves, teaches, and promotes Telugu language, classical literature, and indigenous culture, nurturing cultural identity and linguistic pride among students.
