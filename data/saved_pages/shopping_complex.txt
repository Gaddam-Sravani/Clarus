RGUKT BASAR — CAMPUS SHOPPING COMPLEX & AMENITIES
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
A dedicated Shopping Complex is established inside the RGUKT Basar campus to provide essential goods and commercial services to students, faculty members, and staff. 

PURPOSE & ACCESSIBILITY
The complex offers convenient access to daily necessities and educational services directly within the university grounds, eliminating the need for campus residents to leave the premises for routine requirements.

AVAILABLE ITEMS & SERVICES
1. Daily Essentials & Groceries: General provisions, packaged foods, toiletries, and household necessities.
2. Stationery Supplies: Notebooks, pens, exam pads, drawing instruments, and academic supplies.
3. Reprographic Services: Photocopying (Xerox), printing, and document duplication.
4. Snack Items & Refreshments: Packaged snacks, beverages, and quick bites.
5. Other Daily Needs: Miscellaneous sundries and emergency personal items.
