Hostel Rules & Regulations
1. All movements from and to the hostels should be recorded in the
movement register kept with the security guard at the entrance of the
hostels should be properly signed.
Attendance:
a. The attendance timing for girls at Ladies Hostel is between 10.00
pm & 10.30 pm. Students should record their attendance everyday
without fail.
b. In the case of Men’s hostel all hostellers have to be present in their
respective rooms and keep open the rooms between 10.00 pm &
10.30 pm. Every day to enable the Wardens/Hostel supervisors to
check the hostel rooms.
2. The hostellers should not change rooms on their own. Change of rooms
will be permitted only, after Chief Warden’s approval.
3. The Hostel rooms are subject to inspection by the University / Hostel
authorities to make sure that they are kept neat and tidy and no
unauthorized items like liquor, drugs, lethal weapons etc., are kept in the
room. Possession of any lethal weapons or any instrument / contrivance,
which is likely to cause physical harm to others, is strictly prohibited.
4. Perfect silence is to be maintained in the hostel premises including rooms,
bathrooms, dining halls, corridors, common areas etc., Every student of the
hostel should have the civic responsibility that he or she should not be a
cause for inconvenience, annoyance or disturbance to others.
5. The hostellers should not enter into any unnecessary conversation, quarrel
or altercation with the security personnel posted at the hostels. Use of
abusive, vulgar and unparliamentarily language against the mess staff is
strictly forbidden. Any such complaint received from the staff is subjected
to enquiry and will be viewed seriously.
6. If any hosteller is found indulging in any form of instigation / intimidation /
threat to any other hostellers he / she will be asked to vacate the hostel
forthwith. In this regard, the decision of the Chief Warden is final binding
on the individual’s concerned.

7. Smoking is prohibited in the hostel buildings. Gambling in any form such as
playing cards (even without money at stake), consumption of alcohol, use
of drugs and narcotics and even possession of such things are prohibited.
Anyone found indulging in the use of such things will not be asked to
vacate the hostel but also be rusticated from the University. (Also, students
are expected not to consume alcohol outside and enter the campus.
Suspected students invite corrective action).
8. Celebrating birthday parties inside the campus is strictly prohibited.
9. Hostel students shall not leave the headquarters without prior permission
of the Hostel supervisors/Warden/Chief Warden/Security Officer/DSW.
They shall have to apply for permission in writing in advance stating the
reason for leaving and the application and prior permission from the
concerned authorities shall be deemed to be missing and their
parent/guardian or even the police authorities may be intimated.
10. The inmates of the hostel should not leave the hostel premises on holidays
for the purpose of excursion or panic without prior permission of the
Hostel supervisors/Warden/Chief Warden. However, for any accident of
fatality that may occur during picnic/excursion, the responsibility does not
lie with the institute or Hostel authorities.
11. When leaving the rooms for attending classes or for vacation, etc., fans,
electrical gadgets, lights etc., should be switched off. Glass windows are to
be closed securely.
12. Hostellers coming to the Hostel after the gate closing hours without prior
permission or without valid reason would be imposed with fine. Regular
late comers will not be allowed to stay in the hostel.
13. Water should be carefully used and not wasted.
14. Students coming late / or absent regularly will be expelled from the Hostel.
Such students not be readmitted under any circumstances.
15. The hostellers are not allowed to keep air coolers, musical instruments,
cassette record players, and computers, TV, electric irons or any electrical
equipment inside the hostel. Possession of same will lead to confiscation of
the goods. Cooking in the rooms is strictly not permitted.
16. Students who have finished the six years of course work but yet to
clear backlogs will not be normally given accommodation in the

college hostels. Similarly, students who have not been promoted to the
next higher class and who are not for some reason attending classes during
the given semester or academic year also will not be given accommodation
in the hostels.
17. Hostel inmates who are found guilty of misconduct or guilty of
infringement of any of the rules prescribed above shall be liable to fine,
suspension, rustication or dismissal either from the hostels and/ or from
the institute or both by the appropriate authority.
18. Playing music and videos etc. inside the room is strictly prohibited as it
causes disturbance to the inmates. Any such complaint against boarders
will be viewed very seriously and seizure of the gadgets will be made along
with imposition of heavy fine.
19. Inmates are prohibited from writing slogans or obscene drawings on the
hostel walls and rooms. Heavy penalties will be imposed on
students/group of students indulging in such writings.
20. Female visitors and girl students are not permitted to visit the men’s hostel
any time.
21. All visitors to the hostel including the parents/guardian will have to make
necessary entries in the visitor’s book available at the main entrance with
the security guard.
22. Students should not keep any unauthorized property and unauthorized
person in his/her room.
23. Students are required to use the dustbins for throwing the garbage.
24. No secret activities or meetings are permitted in the hostel premises. For
holding any meeting in the hostel room or anywhere in the hostel area,
prior permission from the Chief Warden/University authorities is essential.
25. The use of motor vehicles such as car, motor cycle, scooters, mopeds, etc.
by student is strictly prohibited inside the college / hostel premises.
26. Students are not permitted to remain in the hostel rooms/ shopping
complex/ground during academic hours.
27. All the hostellers are to note that electrical/ carpentry / plumbing
complaints should be given in writing to the respective warden and the
same is to be entered in the complaint register kept in the office of Chief
warden.
28. All hostel students (Boys & Girls) will have to vacate their rooms before
proceeding for summer vacation.

29. Use of Computers:
a. The usage of laptops is for academic purpose only. Students
should ensure that they do not cause disturbance using laptops
beyond silent hours for fellow students.
b. The University / Hostel authorities will conduct surprise checks
Periodically and if anyone is found violating the above rule,
disciplinary action will be taken him/her.
30 Use of Internet:
a. RGUKT provides internet facility primarily for education purpose
only.
b. Access to external internet usage will be limited to 100 MB per
student per day. This usage does not include use of content
on local server such as browsing video lectures, lecture notes,
internal email etc.
c. Internet usage is a monitored facility. Any student found
browsing/downloading non-educational sites will be subjected
to disciplinary action.
d. Excessive use and misuse of internet will lead to suspension of
internet facility.
e. Every user is responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of
his/her password. Sharing of the same with the other users is not
permitted. Any unauthorized activity originating from a user’s
account shall be the sole responsibility of that user.
31. Use of cell phones:
Ordinary cell phone could be used. But, student should not use them
inside the class rooms, examination halls, Laboratory, Library, offices
and during silence hours in the hostel.
32. Student should visit the hostel office only during visiting hours.
Student should read the circulars put up at all blocks and be
updated with the functioning of the hostel.
33. Violation of any these rules would result in punitive action and
serious violations would be referred to the “Students Disciplinary
Committee”. The decision of the committee would be final.
Guests
34. Guests are not permitted to stay in any of the hostels. If the parents
wish to stay, then the student / parent may approach the respective

warden for accommodation subject to the availability on payment of
nominal charges to be fixed by the University from time-to-time.
35. Parents / guardians are allowed to meet their children only on
Sundays between 9am to 7pm.
36. Relatives and friends of students are not allowed to enter into the
Campus unless they are accompanied by the student’s parents/
authorized guardian.
37. Parents/guardians are not permitted without Identity card.
38. Parents / Guardians visiting the student should remain outside the
entry of hostel. Entry into the hostel blocks is not permitted.
Mess Rules
39. All the students of the hostel should take their food in the allotted
mess only.
40. The meal timings are displayed the entrance of the dining hall.
These timings are to be strictly adhered to. It is not obligatory on the
part of the kitchen / dining hall staff to serve the meals outside the
prescribed timings.
41. The identity card is to be produced at the dining hall, at the time of
breakfast, lunch, tea and dinner, failing which service in the dining hall
is liable to be refused, for want of identification.
42. After eating food, dinner shall leave the cup, plate, waste food etc. in
the designated bins.
43. Dining hall furniture like chairs, tables, etc., and utensils like stainless
steel tumblers, plates, spoons etc., are not to be taken out of the
dining hall. If anyone is found taking these dining hall properties out,
he/she will be suitably penalized.
44. The hostel kitchen is strictly “out of Bounds” for the students/parents
/guardians.
45. Sick persons will be served mess food in the room, only if the student
registers their name with the warden.
46. The hostellers shall not waste food, electricity & water. Wastage of
food is a national loss. If anyone is found indulging in such wastage,
he / she will be asked to vacate the hostel, since wastage causes
unnecessary and unavoidable expenditure to others.
47. Rough handling of dining hall furniture, room furniture or any
furniture/ property or fittings of the hostel is strictly forbidden.

48. The cost of damage will be recovered in the following manner.
a. If any individual or group is identified to have caused the
damage, double the cost will be recovered from
him/his/group.
b. If damage is done in any of the rooms and the person(s) is /
are not identified than double the cost will be recovered from
the room-mates collectively.
c. If a damage is done outside the rooms i.e., in common places
like corridors, bathrooms, recreation halls, mess etc., and the
person(s) is / are not identified, then double the cost will be
recovered, floor wise or block wise or on the whole, as the case
may be. Students found damaging the property will be
expelled from the hostel.
Health Care:
1. 24 hours medical facility is available in the campus.
2. Any hosteller, who is not well, should report to the resident Doctor for
appropriate advice.
3. If the situation warrants, admission to an outside hospital as inpatient, the
same is to be reported to the Chief Warden immediately.
Guidelines for issue of Out-Pass:
49. Out pass would be issued by security officer only upon the
recommendation of the concerned warden.
50. General out-pass (Scholarship, Aadhar, Income certificate, caste certificate,
etc.,) can be obtained 48 hours before only. Emergency out pass will be
issued at any time with substantial evidences.
51. P1, P2 and E1 students are issued out pass only on obtaining permission
from their parents. The parents have to come personally along with
identity card to take their children for outgoing / home.
52. The request for consulting skin, eye and gynecology specialists, the
students have to produce primary approval from the doctor of the campus
health center and the same to be produced to the wardens for approval.
53. Students seeking out pass for undertaking project work have to be
endorsed by the HOD / Project guide / concerned department faculty and
the same to be submitted to the warden for the processing of out pass.

54. Requesting for out pass to visit the temples is not permitted.
55. Students seeking out pass for attending rituals have to take the permission
of warden after due confirmation from the students. However, minor
students have to be accompanied by their parents / authorized persons.
56. Out pass will not be issued on holidays and Sundays. The office will remain
closed.
57. Students are permitted to go to their homes during specified period:
Dasara, Sankranthi, Semester breaks and summer vacation only.
58. Wardens/Hostel supervisors are requested to verify the age of students in
case of E1 students, please ensure that the students should be major (18
years). The out pass should not be issued in case of minors without the
consent of parents/guardian.
59. Students are not permitted to leave the campus between 7 PM to 6 AM
60. Students attending placement drives have to process their request for out
pass through the office of Training and Placement cell.
61. Students requesting for out pass to attend Conferences / Seminar /
Symposiums/ Workshops / Domain related training/ Internships have to be
approved (Signature and Stamp) by their respective HOD/ Guide /
Supervisor and the same to be submitted to the Warden for approval.
62. The Wardens/Hostel supervisors are requested to insist the students to
write the following lines to ensure safety, responsibility and accountability.
“ I __________________________________________________ ( Name , ID
number and Hostel Room No.) is solely responsible for going out on any of the
above mentioned reasons. The Institution is not responsible for my safety out of
the campus.
63. Ragging:
Ragging in any form making unpleasant noise, disorderly conduct, doing any act
which causes or likely to cause physical or psychological harm or raise
apprehension or fear of shame or an embarrassment to the new student, which
include teasing, abusing, playing practical jokes on or causing hurt to such students
or asking the new student to do any or to perform such things and such student
will not in the ordinary course willingly do is strictly forbidden.
