Evaluation Breakdown:
- Theory (100 Marks):
  * Internal (40 Marks): 10 marks for Assignments/Tutorials/Quizzes + 30 marks based on Monthly Tests. Three Monthly Tests (MT-I, MT-II, MT-III) conducted per semester; best 2 out of 3 considered (each MT is 15 marks: 5 marks Part-A objective/short + 10 marks Part-B subjective). At least 3 assignments in Engineering and 6 assignments in PUC.
  * External (60 Marks): End semester examination subjective question paper covering all units.
- Laboratory (100 Marks):
  * Internal (40 Marks): 10 marks Internal Viva-Voce + 30 marks Record, Observation, Attendance, and Lab Performance.
  * External (60 Marks): 20 marks External Viva-Voce + 40 marks Lab Examination experiment.
- Drawing (100 Marks): 25 marks sheets + 25 marks mid-evaluation; 50 marks external manual drawing exam.
- Internship (100 Marks): 30 marks host supervisor evaluation + 30 marks report quality (literature review, methodology, originality) + 40 marks viva-voce and presentation.
- Project Work (100 Marks): 20 marks mid-term presentation & brief report + 40 marks supervisor report evaluation + 40 marks end-semester presentation and viva-voce.
- Non-Credit Courses: Must compulsorily pass; awarded either 'P' (Pass) or 'R' (Remedial).
- Final Year Make-Up Exam: Scheduled within one month of VIII semester results exclusively for B.Tech VIII semester students to complete degree requirements on time.

4. GENERAL EXAMINATION RULES & STUDENT GUIDELINES
1. Hall Ticket & ID Check: Allowed into exam hall only upon presenting Hall Ticket issued by the CoE along with the Institute Permanent Photo ID Card.
2. Reporting & Late Entry Rule: Report at least 15 minutes before the exam starts. Strictly NO student allowed to enter 15 minutes after examination commencement.
3. Exit Rule: No student allowed to leave the hall until the last 30 minutes of the examination.
4. Allowed Items: Students must bring their own pens, pencils, erasers, and non-programmable calculators. Borrowing or exchanging items is strictly forbidden. Programmable calculators and mobile phones are banned.
5. Answer Booklets:
   * Write Student ID, Course Name, Course Code, and Date only on the front page space provided.
   * Writing student name, ID, or personal statements/appeals anywhere else is strictly treated as malpractice.
   * Strike off all blank pages before submitting. Even blank answer booklets must be handed over personally to the invigilator. Once submitted, answer booklets will never be returned.

6. GRADED PUNISHMENTS FOR MALPRACTICE DURING EXAMINATIONS
Nature of Malpractice                                                                                  | Prescribed / Recommended Punishment
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Writing Name/ID at non-designated places, offensive language, or requesting pass marks              | Awarded 'R' grade in the subject concerned (or performance in semester cancelled).
2. Communicating with neighbors inside exam hall                                                       | Awarded 'R' grade in the subject concerned.
3. Possessing incriminating material, copying, exchange of papers, or consulting outside exam hall    | Awarded 'R' grade in the subject and one grade less in all other semester subjects (OR 'R' grade in all subjects of the semester).
4. Impersonation (Proxy exam)                                                                          | Impersonator expelled, debarred, and seat forfeited. Candidate impersonated has all semester exams cancelled, debarred for ONE semester. If outsider, police case filed.
5. Smuggling answer scripts, tearing script, taking script outside, or misbehaving with invigilator    | Expulsion from exam hall, cancellation of performance in all subjects of the semester, and debarred for ONE semester from classwork and exams.
6. Possession of mobile phone / smartwatch / Bluetooth device in exam hall                            | Performance in all semester subjects cancelled, and candidate DETAINED for ONE (1) academic year.
7. Possession of mobile phone / smart device containing relevant subject material                      | Performance in all semester subjects cancelled, and candidate DETAINED for TWO (2) academic years.
8. Transmitting or leaking exam questions/answers via mobile/smart devices or social media             | Performance in all subjects cancelled, candidate DEBARRED permanently from the program (PUC / B.Tech), and criminal case registered.
