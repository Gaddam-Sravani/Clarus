ABOUT RGUKT BASAR — INSTITUTIONAL PROFILE & HISTORY

1. UNIVERSITY OVERVIEW & FOUNDING HISTORY
- Name: Rajiv Gandhi University of Knowledge Technologies (RGUKT), Basar.
- Established: Established in March 2008 by an Act of the Legislature by the then Government of Andhra Pradesh as a full-fledged residential university.
- Original Structure: Designed to admit approximately the top 1% of rural students across three residential campuses: Basar, Nuzvid, and R K Valley (Idupulapaya, Kadapa).
- First Batch: Admitted in August 2008 with approximately 6,000 tenth-class (SSC) passed-out students into a six-year integrated programme.
- State Bifurcation (2015): Following the bifurcation of Andhra Pradesh in 2015, the Basar campus became the only RGUKT university campus in Telangana State.

2. ACCREDITATION & STATUTORY RECOGNITION
- NAAC Accreditation: Accredited by NAAC with a 'C' Grade.
- UGC Recognition: Recognised under Section 2(f) and Section 12B of the University Grants Commission (UGC) Act, 1956.
- Key Metrics:
  * Established: 2008
  * Total Academic Departments: 13 Departments
  * Annual Intake: 1,500+ students per year admitted into Pre-University Course (PUC).
  * Flagship Programme: 6-Year Integrated B.Tech Degree.

3. EDUCATIONAL OBJECTIVES & TEACHING METHODOLOGIES
- Primary Objective: To provide high-quality educational opportunities for talented rural youth of Telangana — specifically the top 1% of rural Class X passed-out students.
- 1:1 Laptop Distribution Policy: Every student admitted to RGUKT Basar is issued a personal laptop from their very first class right through the end of their six-year stay on campus.
- Advanced Cognitive Methodologies:
  * "Learning by Example"
  * "Learning by Doing"
  * "Problem-Based Learning"
  * "Self-Paced Learning"
- Emerging Technology Focus: Disseminates and advances research in Information Technology, Nanotechnology, Cloud Computing, Data Analytics, and the Internet of Things (IoT).
- Broad-Based Education: Includes integrated courses in Humanities, Social Sciences, soft skills, knowledge management, and entrepreneurship.

4. CAMPUS LOCATION, GEOGRAPHY & HERITAGE
- Geographic Location: Situated on the banks of the holy river Godavari, near Nizamabad. Following Telangana's 2016 district reorganization, Basar is a Mandal under Nirmal district administration (Pincode: 504107).
- Historical & Spiritual Heritage: Basar is the home of the historic Gnana Saraswati Temple — dedicated to the Goddess of Knowledge — believed to be one of the two oldest Saraswati temples in India (the other being located in Kashmir).
- 'Akshara Gnana' Ritual: The special ceremony of 'Akshara Gnana' (initiating children into learning and writing) draws devotees from across the nation to this sacred site.
- Connectivity: The campus is well connected to all parts of India by both road and rail routes.
