CAMPUS SUB-POST OFFICE — RGUKT BASAR
Location: Sub-Post Office, RGUKT Basar Campus, Basar — 504107, Nirmal District, Telangana.

1. POSTAL OVERVIEW & PIN CODE
- Campus Facility: RGUKT Basar has an operational Sub-Post Office within the campus for all students, faculty, and administrative staff.
- Official Postal PIN Code: 504107 (District: Nirmal, Telangana).
  * Use PIN Code 504107 for all incoming and outgoing personal, academic, and administrative campus mail/parcels.

2. WORKING HOURS & SCHEDULE
- Operating Days: Monday to Saturday.
- Daily Timings: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM.
- Holidays: Closed on Sundays and all Gazetted Public Holidays.

3. POSTAL & FINANCIAL SERVICES AVAILABLE
The campus sub-post office provides the following 7 core services:
1. Speed Post and Registered Post booking.
2. Parcel and courier booking and delivery.
3. Money Order and Electronic Money Order (eMO) transmission.
4. Post Office Savings Bank (POSB) accounts.
5. Recurring Deposit (RD) and Fixed Deposit (FD) savings schemes.
6. Postal Life Insurance (PLI) services.
7. Retail sale of postage stamps, envelopes, postcards, and inland letters.
