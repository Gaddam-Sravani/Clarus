RGUKT BASAR — EXAMINATION FEES, CERTIFICATE FEES & TC (TRANSFER CERTIFICATE) FEES
================================================================================
Official Schedule of University Examination Fees, Remedial Fees, Revaluation Fees, Transcript Charges, and Transfer Certificate (TC) Fees:

EXAMINATION FEES & CERTIFICATE CHARGES — RGUKT BASAR
Authority: Office of the Controller of Examinations (CoE) / Director, RGUKT Basar.

1. EXAMINATION & REVALUATION FEE SCHEDULE
Candidates must apply in the prescribed form and pay the prescribed fee into the bank in favor of "The Director, RGUKT-Basar" on or before the announced deadline:
- End Semester Examination (ESE): Rs. 500/- per semester
- Remedial Examination: Rs. 100/- per subject
- Grade Improvement Examination: Rs. 100/- per subject
- Revaluation Fee: Rs. 100/- per subject
- Photocopy of Theory Answer Script: Rs. 1,000/- per subject (Students desiring revaluation should apply separately without waiting for the photocopy).

2. CERTIFICATE FEES & VERIFICATION CHARGES
- Consolidated Marks Memo / Transcript: Rs. 50/- each
- Semester-Wise Transcript: Rs. 20/- each
- Student Educational Verification: Rs. 500/- each
- Correction in any Certificate: Rs. 75/- each
- Original Degree Certificate: Rs. 150/-
- Any Correction in or Re-issue of Degree Certificate: Rs. 150/-
- Transfer Certificate (TC): Rs. 50/-
- Migration Certificate: Rs. 50/-
- Duplicate / Replacement Identity Card: Rs. 50/-
