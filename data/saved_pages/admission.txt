I. ADMISSION & II. DURATION OF PROGRAMME — RGUKT BASAR
Rules and Regulations for the Six-Year Integrated B.Tech Programme (w.e.f. Academic Year 2016–17)

1. ADMISSION ELIGIBILITY & SEAT RESERVATION (SECTION I)
1.1 Merit & Deprivation Score Weightage:
- Admissions to the 1st year of the 6-Year Integrated B.Tech Program are strictly based on merit in the Grade Point Average (GPA) and Grade obtained in each subject of the SSC (10th class) or equivalent recognized examination, following statutory reservations.
- Deprivation Score of 0.4: As prescribed under Statute 13(3), an additional weightage of 0.4 score is added to the 10th class GPA of applicants who studied in non-residential Government Schools (including Zilla Parishad and Municipal Schools) to support socio-economically challenged rural students.

1.2 State Quota & Reservation Ratio (85% Local / 15% Unreserved):
- 85% Local Candidates Quota: 85% of total available seats at RGUKT Basar are strictly reserved for Local Candidates of Telangana State.
- 15% Unreserved Quota: The remaining 15% of seats are unreserved and filled based on merit with students from both Telangana State and Andhra Pradesh, in compliance with Presidential Order 371 Article D and Section 95 of the A.P. Reorganization Act, 2014.


2. DURATION OF PROGRAMME & SEMESTER STRUCTURE (SECTION II)
2.1 Programme Architecture:
- The Six-Year Integrated Program comprises twelve (12) semesters across two parts:
  * Part 1: 2-Year Pre-University Course (PUC) of 4 semesters.
  * Part 2: 4-Year Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) course of 8 semesters.
- Semester Breakdown: Each semester comprises:
  * Instruction Period: 16 weeks
  * Preparation Holidays & Practical Examinations: 2 weeks

2.2 Admissions Cutoff & Fee Refund Policy:
- Counseling Cutoff: No admissions are made after counseling is concluded. In case of vacant seats, admission is granted based on merit within four (4) weeks of the commencement of instruction.
- Readmission / Promotion Cutoff: No readmission or promotion is allowed after four (4) weeks of commencement of instruction.
- Court Cases: Candidates admitted under court directives after the cutoff date have their admission reserved for the subsequent year on a supernumerary basis.
- No Tuition Fee Refund: No refund of tuition fee will be made for students withdrawing their admission after the commencement of instruction.

2.3 Available B.Tech Disciplines:
Upon completing PUC, candidates are admitted into one of the 7 four-year B.Tech programs based on merit (CGPA) and statutory reservations:
1. Chemical Engineering
2. Civil Engineering
3. Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
4. Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE)
5. Electronics and Communications Engineering (ECE)
6. Mechanical Engineering (ME)
7. Metallurgical and Materials Engineering (MME)

2.4 MAXIMUM TIME LIMITS FOR DEGREE COMPLETION (CLAUSE 4)
A candidate must fulfill all graduation requirements within these strict time boundaries:
a) 2-Year PUC Limit: Must complete the 2-Year PUC within a maximum period of three (3) years from admission.
b) 4-Year B.Tech Limit: Must complete the 4-Year B.Tech course within a maximum period of six (6) years from branch allocation.
c) Overall Degree Limit: Must fulfill all requirements for the award of the B.Tech degree within an absolute maximum of eight (8) academic years from the date of admission.
- Forfeiture Rule: If a student fails to satisfy either condition (a), (b), or (c), they forfeit their seat and their admission stands cancelled automatically.
