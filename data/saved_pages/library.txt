CENTRAL LIBRARY & KNOWLEDGE RESOURCE CENTRE — RGUKT BASAR
=================================================================

1. OVERVIEW & INFRASTRUCTURE
The Central Library of RGUKT Basar was established in 2008 and serves over 9,000 students and faculty members. It is housed in a dedicated building providing study spaces and individual reading carrels.
- Holdings: 60,000+ Books and Volumes, 100+ Print Journals, and 10,000+ E-Books and E-Journals.
- Collections: Prescribed textbooks for all PUC and B.Tech engineering branches, advanced reference books, peer-reviewed journals, and student project theses/dissertations.

2. DIGITAL RESOURCES & E-LEARNING ACCESS
The digital library section provides 150+ networked terminals with access to national and international databases:
- NPTEL: Video lectures by IIT and IISc faculty.
- SWAYAM: National MOOCs platform for credit-transfer courses.
- DELNET: Developing Library Network for interlibrary loans and resource sharing.
- NDL India: National Digital Library of India.
- Shodhganga: Repository of Indian electronic theses and dissertations.
- e-ShodhSindhu: University consortium for peer-reviewed research journals.

6. RULES, FINES & NO-DUE CLEARANCE PROCESS
- Book Returns & Late Fine: A fine of Rs. 200/- is charged for late submission of borrowed books.
- Mandatory 'No Due' Clearance: Obtaining a 'No Due' clearance certificate from the Central Library is compulsory for all graduating or exiting students before vacating the campus.
- Clearance Desk Location: Central Library Building, 3rd Floor.
- Official Contact Email: library@rgukt.ac.in
- Campus Address: Central Library Building, RGUKT Basar, Basara, Nirmal District, Telangana – 504107.
