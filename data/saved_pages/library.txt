About the Library
The Central Library of RGUKT Basar is the premier knowledge resource centre of the university, serving over 9,000 students and faculty. Established alongside the university in 2008, it has grown into a well-equipped facility with a comprehensive collection of textbooks, reference books, journals, and digital resources to support all academic programmes.
The library is housed in a dedicated building providing a calm and conducive environment for study and research. It subscribes to several national and international databases and provides access to thousands of e-journals and e-books.
