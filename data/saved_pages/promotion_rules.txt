X. RULES OF PROMOTION (PUC & B.TECH) — RGUKT BASAR

1. RULES OF PROMOTION FOR PRE-UNIVERSITY COURSE (PUC)
1.1 Promotion from PUC to B.Tech 1st Year (E1):
- Academic Performance: Students entering the B.Tech program must secure a CGPA of 6.00 or more (CGPA >= 6.00) by the end of the 4th semester of the PUC program.
- Backlogs: Must have zero backlogs; all subjects from the 1st to the 4th semester of PUC must be cleared.

1.2 Remedial & Grade Improvement Examinations in PUC:
- Opportunity: Students failing to fulfill promotion criteria to B.Tech E1 can write remedial examinations in failed subjects and grade improvement examinations in any number of passed subjects.
- Exam Structure: Remedial and grade improvement students write only the End Semester Examination for 60 marks. The Continuous Internal Assessment (40 marks) is carried over from the regular semester (no separate internal test).
- Grade Improvement Limits:
  * Maximum of two (2) chances per subject for students with < 50% marks (D Grade) to improve overall CGPA.
  * Maximum of one (1) chance per subject for students with >= 50% marks (D Grade) to improve overall CGPA.
- Final Grading: The best grade obtained among all attempts in a given subject is included in the final marks sheet and CGPA calculation.
- Schedule: Conducted after the regular semester examinations.

1.3 PUC Exit Certificate Policy (For Exiting at the End of PUC-II):
- Minimum pass marks per subject: 40% (P-Grade).
- Students passing all subjects with a CGPA >= 5.00 can either exit with a PUC completion certificate or attempt grade improvement to become eligible for E1 promotion.
- Maximum Time Limits:
  * The 2-year PUC program must be completed within a maximum period of three (3) years to become eligible for promotion into the 4-Year B.Tech program.
  * Students failing to meet B.Tech promotion criteria but satisfying PUC pass requirements must exit the university, provided they pass all PUC courses within a maximum period of four (4) years from admission.


2. RULES OF PROMOTION FOR B.TECH (ADMITTED FROM ACADEMIC YEAR 2016-17 ONWARDS)

S.No | Transition Phase / Semester            | Conditions to be Fulfilled for Promotion
-----|----------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1    | 1st Semester to 2nd Semester (E1 S1->S2)| Regular program of study of B.Tech 1st Semester.
2    | 2nd Semester to 3rd Semester (E1->E2)  | a. Regular program of study of B.Tech 2nd Semester.<br>b. Must earn at least 50% of credits prescribed for B.Tech 1st & 2nd Semesters combined.
3    | 3rd Semester to 4th Semester (E2 S1->S2)| Regular program of study of B.Tech 3rd Semester.
4    | 4th Semester to 5th Semester (E2->E3)  | a. Regular program of study of B.Tech 4th Semester.<br>b. Backlog credits across 1st, 2nd, 3rd, and 4th Semesters combined shall not exceed 50% of total credits prescribed for 3rd & 4th Semesters.
5    | 5th Semester to 6th Semester (E3 S1->S2)| Regular program of study of B.Tech 5th Semester.
6    | 6th Semester to 7th Semester (E3->E4)  | a. Regular program of study of B.Tech 6th Semester.<br>b. Backlog credits across 1st to 6th Semesters combined shall not exceed 50% of total credits prescribed for 5th & 6th Semesters.
7    | 7th Semester to 8th Semester (E4 S1->S2)| Regular program of study of B.Tech 7th Semester.

Permitted Backlog Credits Breakdown:
- Prescribed 43/44 credits -> Permitted as backlogs: 22 credits
- Prescribed 45/46 credits -> Permitted as backlogs: 23 credits
- Prescribed 47/48 credits -> Permitted as backlogs: 24 credits
- Prescribed 49/50 credits -> Permitted as backlogs: 25 credits
*(Note: If permitted backlog credits turn out to be a fraction, credits are rounded to the next higher digit).*

Detention Rule for B.Tech:
- If a candidate carries more than the permitted number of backlog credits, they are DETAINED.
- Readmission: Detained candidates taking readmission must pay the total fee for that year, and all credits earned during that year become null and void.

Post-B.Tech CGPA Improvement:
- Students may improve their overall CGPA within one (1) academic year immediately after passing all B.Tech examinations, by reappearing at not more than two semesters (all subjects of the semester taken together). The higher of the two aggregates/grades is awarded.


3. RULES OF PROMOTION FOR B.TECH (ADMITTED BEFORE ACADEMIC YEAR 2016-17)
- 1st Year to 2nd Year: Student must have passed all subjects (theory and labs) of B.Tech 1st Year.
- 2nd Year to 3rd Year & 3rd Year to 4th Year: No credit restriction. However, the student must complete the 6-Year Integrated B.Tech Programme (PUC + B.Tech) within a maximum period of eight (8) years from the date of admission (including 2 additional grace years).
- Degree Requirement: Minimum CGPA of 6.00 or more by the end of the 4th year of B.Tech.
