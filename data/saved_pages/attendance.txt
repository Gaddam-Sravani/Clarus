IV. RULES AND REGULATIONS OF ATTENDANCE — RGUKT BASAR
Applicable to all students across the 6-Year Integrated B.Tech Programme (PUC and B.Tech)

1. REGULAR PROGRAMME OF STUDY REQUIREMENT
- Candidates admitted to a particular program of study are required to pursue a "Regular Program of Study" before they are permitted to appear for the University Examination.
- "A regular program of study" strictly means putting in attendance of not less than 75% in aggregate.

2. CONDONATION OF ATTENDANCE DEFICIENCY ON MEDICAL GROUNDS
a) Standard Medical Condonation (Up to 10%):
   - In special cases and for valid medical reasons, the Vice-Chancellor may, on the specific recommendation of the Director / Head of the Department (HOD), condone the deficiency in attendance to the extent of 10% on medical grounds (i.e., attendance between 65% and 74.9%).
   - Subject to submission of a genuine medical certificate and payment of the prescribed condonation fee.
b) Condonation for Women Candidates (Pregnancy - Up to 15%):
   - In respect of women candidates who seek condonation of attendance due to pregnancy, the Vice-Chancellor may condone the deficiency in attendance to the extent of 15% (as against 10% condonation for others) on medical grounds (i.e., attendance down to 60%).
   - Subject to submission of a valid medical certificate.
   - Frequency: Such condonation due to pregnancy shall not be granted more than once during the entire 6-year program of study.

3. CONDONATION FEE AMOUNT & PAYMENT METHOD
- The fee for condonation of attendance on medical grounds is Rs. 500/-.
- Payment Mode: Payable through Demand Draft (DD) drawn or Bank challan paid in favor of "The Director, RGUKT-Basar".

4. PROLONGED MEDICAL ABSENCE & ROLL REMOVAL RULES
- Medical Absence up to 1 Academic Year: May be granted upon submission of genuine documents and a signed undertaking by the student and parent/guardian that the absence is not taken for undergoing entrance test coaching or joining elsewhere, subject to approval of the Dean (Academics).
- Absence Exceeding 1 Year (Removal from Rolls): A student's name will be permanently removed from the rolls if the medical absence exceeds one (1) academic year.
- Special Exception (1 to 2 Years Absence): In genuine cases, an absence of more than one year and up to a maximum period of two (2) years can be granted by the Academic Council on prior application, ONLY to those students who are studying beyond the first year of Engineering (E2, E3, or E4).

5. OFFICIAL CO-CURRICULAR & DUTY LEAVE (NCC / NSS / SPORTS)
- Attendance of NCC/NSS camps, Inter-Collegiate, Inter-University, Inter-State, or International matches, debates, or Educational Excursions (or other approved university activities involving travel outside the city where the college is situated) will NOT be counted as absence.
- Limitations:
  * Such duty absence shall not exceed two (2) weeks per semester of the total period of instruction.
  * Such leave can be availed only once during the entire 6-Year integrated programme of study.

6. ATTENDANCE CALCULATION
- Attendance is calculated on the aggregate of all papers/courses from the date of commencement of classes (or date of readmission in case of detained candidates) as per the university almanac.

7. DETENTION & READMISSION RULES
- General Detention: Candidates who fail to secure the required attendance in a program of study shall be DETAINED in the same class and will not be permitted to appear for the University Examination.
- Readmission: Detained candidates must seek readmission into the same class during the subsequent academic year after fulfilling attendance requirements and paying the requisite fee.

8. THE 40% ATTENDANCE THRESHOLD & CANCELLATION OF ADMISSION (FROM AY 2016-17)
Applicable to candidates admitted to the first year of PUC (PUC-I) or first year of 4-Year B.Tech (E1):
a) Attendance between 40% and 74.9%:
   - Candidates who do not have the requisite attendance, but have secured NOT LESS THAN 40% attendance, are eligible to seek readmission into the same class during the subsequent year upon payment of the requisite fee.
b) Attendance LESS THAN 40% (Forfeiture of Seat):
   - PUC-I Students: Candidates admitted to the first year of PUC who fail to secure the minimum 40% attendance will LOSE their admission completely and must leave the university with their SSC (10th class) certificates only.
   - B.Tech E1 Students: Candidates admitted to the first year of B.Tech (E1) after PUC who fail to secure the minimum 40% attendance will LOSE their admission from the concerned B.Tech program and must leave the university with their PUC certificates.
