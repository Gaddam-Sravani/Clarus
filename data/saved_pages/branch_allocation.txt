RGUKT BASAR — B.TECH BRANCH ALLOCATION RULES & ENGINEERING SEAT ALLOCATION
================================================================================
Official Regulations for Allocation of Engineering Branches, Selection Cutoffs (CGPA 6.00), and Branch Seat Quotas for PUC Students:

III. ALLOCATION OF SEATS IN ENGINEERING BRANCHES — RGUKT BASAR

1. MINIMUM ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA FOR ENGINEERING BRANCH ALLOCATION
- An engineering branch will be allocated ONLY to those students who pass the 2-Year Pre-University Course (PUC) with a minimum Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 6.00 and above (CGPA >= 6.00).
- The allocation is evaluated considering the remedial examinations after the 2nd Semester of 2nd Year PUC.

2. SEAT ALLOCATION PROCEDURE
i. Online Preference Collection: Student choices in order of preference (ranking up to all 7 available engineering branches) are collected online.
ii. Merit & Category Order: RGUKT takes the overall CGPA of all students meeting the minimum CGPA >= 6.00 threshold, arranges them in order of merit within each statutory reservation category, and allocates seats according to merit order and available branch capacity.

3. TIE-BREAKING CRITERIA (IN CASE OF EQUAL CGPA)
If two or more candidates secure the exact same overall CGPA, the relative ranking tie is resolved sequentially based on:
1. First Criterion: Average grade point secured in Mathematics courses across all four (4) semesters of PUC.
2. Second Criterion: Average grade point secured in Physics courses across all four (4) semesters of PUC.
3. Third Criterion: If the tie still persists, the older candidate (determined by Date of Birth) will be given preference over the younger candidate.
