4. GENERAL RULES AND REGULATIONS OF SPORTS
1. Players should report to the ground before 15 mins.
2. Players should report the ground with sports dress along with shoes.
3. Players should obey referee’s decision.
4. Players should respect each and every player irrespective of branches and should not use any vulgar language.
5. Team captain should only take the lead to discuss with referee in case of any issues regarding the game.
6. Indiscipline activities are not allowed during the game.
7. If there are any substitutions in the team during the match, captain should inform to the referee.
(Signed by: Dr. B. Vinod, Convener of Sports and Games)

5. RULES AND REGULATIONS OF THE BADMINTON ARENA (VERBATIM 1 TO 23)
1. The badminton arena will be open on all working days in both the sessions. The timings of the sessions are 5:30 am – 7:30 am and 5:30 pm – 8:00 pm for both boy students and girl students.
2. Proper sports attire (Shorts or track pants, sports t-shirt or sleeveless, and badminton non-marking shoes) is a must to play on the wooden courts.
3. Don’t drop sweat on the wooden floor.
4. Spilling water on the wooden floor is strictly prohibited.
5. Always carry a dry towel or a handkerchief to wipe your sweat.
6. Do not bring water bottles near to the court as it could spill water on the court. Keep the water bottles outside the badminton arena.
7. Bring your own racquets and shuttles when you come to play.
8. Do not practice in between the courts. Do not disturb other players when they play.
9. One court is reserved for girl students, two courts are reserved for boy students – one for learners and one for professionals, and one court is reserved for the staff.
10. If the courts reserved for a particular group is empty anyone can play but should vacate when they come.
11. If a court is crowded with players, losers out will be followed and no winning team can play more than three matches of one set each.
12. The courts can be occupied in first come first serve basis and the same rotation should be followed.
13. One should warm up one’s body before play and cool it down after the play. Warming up and cooling down must not be done on the badminton courts.
14. Do not use mobile phones or play music in badminton arena. Keep your mobiles in silent mode or place them near the gallery when you come to the badminton arena.
15. When branch games are conducted only one person from each branch playing is allowed to be near the court to give suggestions and to call time outs. The rest of the members must sit in the gallery and meet the players only during the timeouts and breaks only. If a team fails to follow this the entire team will be disqualified.
16. During branch games and staff leagues referee’s decision will be final. If any team argues with the referee or the line umpires then the entire team will be disqualified.
17. If anyone wants to conduct a badminton tournament, prior permission of the sports convener must be sought.
18. Sports convener reserves the right to add, modify or delete any rules depending on the circumstances.
19. You are responsible for your own belongings.
20. Please make sure that the lights are switched off if you are the last to leave the arena.
21. Do not litter and keep the badminton arena clean.
22. Do not involve in unnecessary activities in the badminton arena.
23. Those who do not follow the etiquettes of the badminton arena are liable for strict punishment.

6. RULES OF KHO-KHO GROUND
1. No one is allowed to walk on the kho-kho ground in the day time and night time as well.
2. No one should throw waste or any plastic material in the ground.
3. No one should play or practice on the ground and also its premises.
4. No one should conduct any other activities without permission of sports convener.
5. No one should play volleyball and cricket near the ground.
Penalty: If any student found violating the rules mentioned by the sports department serious action will be taken and also penalty of Rs. 500/- will be charged.
(Signed by: Dr. B. Vinod, Convener of Sports and Games)

7. HUB NOTICE: GROUND RULES AND REGULATIONS
1. No one is allowed to play or practice on under constructing basketball ground (any other activities without permission). If anyone found or recognized by security or sports department they will be fined with penalty of Rs. 500/-.
2. No one is allowed to play and wander in or around ground after 8:00 pm. If any one found he/she will be dismissed from their respective department game and action will be taken.
3. No one is allowed to eat and throw waste in ground and its premises if found, serious actions will be taken by the administration.
4. No game practice should be done during academic hours.
