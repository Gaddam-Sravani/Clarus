RGUKT BASAR — REMEDIAL EXAMINATION RULES, BACKLOG EXAMS & SUPPLEMENTARY REGULATIONS
================================================================================
Official Rules for Remedial Examinations, Backlog Clearance, R-Grade Removal, and Academic Standing:

XI. REMEDIAL EXAMINATION RULES & GRADING POLICIES — RGUKT BASAR

1. MANDATORY CLEARANCE OF REMEDIAL (R) GRADES
Students securing a remedial grade ('R' grade) in any course must clear their backlogs by registering for and appearing in remedial/supplementary examinations whenever announced by the University.

2. PUC TO B.TECH PROMOTION RESTRICTION
Promotion from the Pre-University Course (PUC) into the 4-Year B.Tech engineering stream will strictly NOT be permitted for students who have pending remedial backlogs in PUC. All PUC courses must be cleared.

3. WITHHOLDING OF OFFICIAL CERTIFICATES
No certificate—neither Provisional Certificate nor Original Degree Certificate—can be issued by the University to students who have pending backlogs in their PUC stream. Only an interim marks statement may be issued upon application.

4. ASTERISK (*) MARK ON MARKS MEMORANDUM
For all students clearing subjects through remedial examinations, the marks memorandum will reflect the grade earned in that subject marked with an asterisk (*). For example, earning a 'B' grade in a remedial attempt will be officially displayed as 'B*'.

5. GRADE POINT PENALTY (ONE GRADE LOWER FOR SGPA / CGPA)
For the purpose of calculating SGPA and CGPA, the grade point corresponding to ONE GRADE LOWER than the grade earned in the remedial examination will be awarded:
- If a student earns an 'Ex' grade (10 pts) in remedial -> Awarded grade points of 'A' grade (9 pts).
- If a student earns an 'A' grade (9 pts) in remedial -> Awarded grade points of 'B' grade (8 pts).
- If a student earns a 'B' grade (8 pts) in remedial -> Awarded grade points of 'C' grade (7 pts).
- If a student earns a 'C' grade (7 pts) in remedial -> Awarded grade points of 'D' grade (6 pts).
- Floor Limit: The grade point of the lowest pass grade ('P' grade or 'D' grade as per applicability) will NOT be lowered further.
