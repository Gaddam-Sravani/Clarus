V. REGULATIONS FOR GRANTING WITHDRAWAL ON MEDICAL GROUNDS AND TAKING RE-ADMISSION — RGUKT BASAR

1. TEMPORARY WITHDRAWAL CONDITIONS & PROCEDURE
A student may be permitted to withdraw temporarily for a period of one (1) semester or more from the university on the grounds of prolonged illness or acute problem in the family which compelled him/her to stay at home, provided:
a) Application Deadline: The student applies to the University within 15 days of the commencement of the semester or from the date he/she last attended classes, whichever is later, stating fully the reasons for withdrawal together with supporting medical documents and endorsement of the father/guardian.
b) Degree Time Limit: Permission is granted if the Institute is satisfied that, inclusive of the period of withdrawal, the student is likely to complete degree requirements within the stipulated time limits.
c) Undertaking: The student must submit an undertaking letter duly signed by the student and parent/guardian indicating that the absence was not taken for undergoing entrance test coaching or joining elsewhere, with the approval of the Dean (Academics).
d) Re-Admission Batch: A student granted medical withdrawal shall seek re-admission into the same semester along with subsequent year students.
e) Lifetime Limit: A student will be granted only one (1) such temporary withdrawal during his/her entire tenure as a student of the University.

2. MEDICAL BOARD APPROVAL
- Long absence cases on medical grounds must be formally recommended by a Medical Board under the chairmanship of the Dean (Academics).
- Students must seek prior permission before discontinuing classes.

3. FEES AND CHARGES FOR RE-ADMITTED STUDENTS
Students who are readmitted on medical grounds or other extraordinary grounds are liable for the following fee schedule:
- Mess and Accommodation Charges: Must pay mess and accommodation charges of Rs. 100/- per day for the period of days present during the semester in which the student discontinued.
- Re-Admission Registration Fee:
  * General / OBC Categories: Rs. 1,000/-
  * SC and ST Categories: Rs. 500/-
  *(Payable irrespective of the semester in which the candidate seeks readmission).*
- Regular Annual Tuition Fee:
  * PUC Programmes: Rs. 36,000/- per year
  * B.Tech Programmes: Rs. 40,000/- per year
  * Plus any other miscellaneous fees (examination fees, etc., as applicable).
