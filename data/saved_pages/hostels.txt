Hostel Facility
The residential nature of RGUKT — where students come from a geographically distributed wide area and would not be able to commute to the campus daily — makes it necessary to provide hostel facilities for all students.
RGUKT, Basar has a total of
6 hostel blocks
— 3 each for boys and girls separately. Between them, the hostel buildings can accommodate approximately
8,000 students
.
Administration
Each hostel is provided with a caretaker to look after students' problems. The welfare of students is monitored by a group of wardens who all report to a
Chief Warden
. There are separate wardens and chief wardens for girls and boys.
Room Amenities
Every room in the hostel is fitted with fans and lighting. Each student is provided with:
A cot with a mattress
Bed sheets and pillows
Towels
Sports and regular uniforms
Shared Facilities
Common washing and toilet areas in each wing
Daily cleaning by housekeeping staff for a clean and hygienic environment
Special halls in each hostel for reading and recreation
Shuttle court at the centre of each hostel building for recreation
Wi-Fi Connectivity
A notable feature of the hostels is that they are
completely Wi-Fi enabled
, albeit with controlled access. This ensures students find no interruption in their efforts to acquire knowledge and keep up with class work and assignments.
