HOSTEL FACILITIES, RULES & REGULATIONS, AND OUT-PASS GUIDELINES — RGUKT BASAR

1. HOSTEL INFRASTRUCTURE & CAPACITY
- Campus Structure: Fully residential campus.
- Capacity & Blocks: 6 hostel blocks in total (3 blocks for boys and 3 blocks for girls), accommodating approximately 8,000 students.
- Administration: Supervised by caretakers, wardens, and Chief Wardens reporting to the Associate Dean of Student Welfare (ADSW).
- Room Amenities Provided: Each room is fitted with fans and lighting. Every student is provided with:
  * A cot with a mattress
  * Bed sheets and pillows
  * Towels
  * Sports and regular uniforms
- Shared Facilities: Common washing and hygienic toilet areas in each wing, daily housekeeping, dedicated reading and recreation halls, and a shuttle badminton court at the centre of each hostel block.
- Wi-Fi Access: Completely Wi-Fi enabled with controlled, monitored educational access.

2. HOSTEL DISCIPLINE & DAILY TIMINGS
1. Movement Register: All movements to and from hostels must be recorded and signed in the register with the security guard at the hostel entrance.
2. Night Attendance (10:00 PM – 10:30 PM):
   * Girls Hostel: Mandatory attendance recorded every day between 10:00 PM and 10:30 PM.
   * Boys Hostel: All hostellers must be present in their respective rooms with doors kept open between 10:00 PM and 10:30 PM for roll call verification by Wardens/Supervisors.
3. Room Changes: Changing rooms without approval is strictly prohibited; permitted only after the Chief Warden's written approval.
4. Inspections & Prohibited Items: Authorities conduct surprise checks. Possession of liquor, alcohol, drugs, narcotics, gambling cards, lethal weapons, or any instrument likely to cause physical harm is strictly prohibited and results in immediate rustication/police intimation. Students are prohibited from entering campus intoxicated.
5. Silence Hours: Strict silence must be maintained in rooms, bathrooms, corridors, and dining halls.
6. Prohibited Celebrations: Celebrating birthday parties inside the campus is strictly prohibited.
7. Electrical Equipment Bans: Air coolers, musical instruments, cassette players, computers, TVs, electric irons, or cooking appliances are strictly prohibited and will be confiscated. Cooking in rooms is forbidden.
8. Summer Vacation: All students (Boys & Girls) must vacate rooms before leaving for summer vacation.
9. Motor Vehicles: Use of cars, motorcycles, scooters, or mopeds by students is strictly prohibited inside college/hostel premises.
10. Academic Hours Restriction: Students are strictly not permitted to remain in hostel rooms, grounds, or the shopping complex during academic hours.
11. Maintenance Complaints: Electrical, carpentry, and plumbing complaints must be written in the complaint register at the Chief Warden's office.

3. DIGITAL USAGE & GADGET POLICIES
- Laptop Usage: Permitted for academic purposes only; must not cause disturbance beyond silent hours.
- Internet Limit: External internet access is limited to 100 MB per student per day (usage does not count local server content like lecture notes, video lectures, and internal email). Monitored facility; non-educational browsing results in suspension. Sharing account passwords is strictly prohibited.
- Mobile Phones: Ordinary mobile phones are permitted, but strictly prohibited inside classrooms, exam halls, labs, library, offices, and during hostel silence hours.

4. GUEST & PARENT VISITING RULES
- Overnight Guests: Guests are not permitted to stay in hostels. Parents may request temporary accommodation through the warden subject to availability on payment of nominal charges.
- Visiting Days & Hours: Parents/guardians are permitted to meet their children ONLY on Sundays between 9:00 AM and 7:00 PM.
- Visiting Location: Parents must remain outside the hostel entrance and show their ID card. Entry into student rooms/blocks is forbidden. Unaccompanied relatives/friends are not permitted.

5. MESS & DINING RULES
1. Food must be taken only in the allotted mess.
2. Identity Card: Must be produced at the dining hall for breakfast, lunch, snacks/tea, and dinner.
3. Utensils & Property: Chairs, tables, plates, stainless steel tumblers, and spoons must not be removed from the dining hall. Kitchens are strictly out of bounds.
4. Sick Students: Served mess food in their room only if registered with the warden.
5. Damage Recovery Policy:
   * Identified Individual/Group: Double the replacement cost recovered from them.
   * Inside a Room (Unidentified): Double the cost recovered from all roommates collectively.
   * Common Areas (Corridors, Mess, Bathrooms): Double the cost recovered floor-wise, block-wise, or hostel-wide, and culprits expelled.

6. HEALTHCARE EMERGENCIES
- 24-hour campus medical facility available.
- Inpatient referral to an outside hospital must be reported to the Chief Warden immediately.

7. GUIDELINES FOR ISSUE OF OUT-PASS
- General Rules:
  * Issued by the Security Officer only upon recommendation of the concerned Warden.
  * Campus Check-out Hours: 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM (Students are not permitted to leave campus between 7:00 PM and 6:00 AM).
  * Out-pass office timings: 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM (Closed on Sundays and holidays).
  * Visiting temples is not a valid ground for out-pass.
  * Permitted home visit periods: Dasara, Sankranthi, semester breaks, and summer vacation only.

8. ANTI-RAGGING WARNING (CLAUSE 63)
Ragging in any form (making unpleasant noise, disorderly conduct, physical/psychological harm, teasing, abusing, or forcing tasks) is strictly forbidden and punishable by immediate expulsion, fine, and police prosecution.
