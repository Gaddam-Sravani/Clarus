About the Department
Established 2010
The Computer Science and Engineering department was established in 2010. It provides a comprehensive research setting with teaching excellence. The department employs qualified faculty who encourage student engagement in extracurricular activities. The curriculum emphasizes developing strong technical expertise while maintaining a holistic approach to staff and student growth. The institution prioritizes research initiatives, paper publications, and awards alongside practical skill development through modern facilities and industry-standard technologies.
Vision & Mission
Vision
To be a globally recognized centre for excellence in the arena of Computer Science & Engineering education and research, catering to the current needs of industry and the society by producing globally competent and quality computer professionals.
Mission
Producing computer science graduates with strong subject knowledge and certified skill-sets to lead in rapidly evolving fields. Strengthening partnerships with multinational companies to keep students current with technological advancements. Developing professional behavior and ethical leadership in students to serve society with commitment. Providing practical exposure across various domains and undertaking research activities. Building entrepreneurship and managerial capabilities through world-class engineering and management education.
