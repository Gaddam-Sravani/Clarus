DEPARTMENT OF COMPUTER SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING (CSE) — RGUKT BASAR

1. DEPARTMENT OVERVIEW & PROGRAMMES
- Established: 2010
- Total Faculty Members: 23
- Academic Programme Offered: Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Computer Science and Engineering.
- Core Focus: Provides a comprehensive research setting with teaching excellence, emphasizing strong technical expertise, paper publications, practical skill development, and modern laboratory facilities.
- Location: Department of Computer Science and Engineering, RGUKT Basar, Basar — 504107, Nirmal District, Telangana.

2. VISION & MISSION
- Vision:
  To be a globally recognized centre for excellence in the arena of Computer Science & Engineering education and research, catering to the current needs of industry and the society by producing globally competent and quality computer professionals.
- Mission:
  * Producing computer science graduates with strong subject knowledge and certified skill-sets to lead in rapidly evolving fields.
  * Strengthening partnerships with multinational companies to keep students current with technological advancements.
  * Developing professional behavior and ethical leadership in students to serve society with commitment.
  * Providing practical exposure across various domains and undertaking research activities.
  * Building entrepreneurship and managerial capabilities through world-class engineering and management education.
