CAMPUS HOSPITAL & MEDICAL HEALTH CENTRE — RGUKT BASAR
Location: Health Centre / Campus Hospital, RGUKT Basar, Basar — 504107, Nirmal District, Telangana.

1. MEDICAL CENTRE INFRASTRUCTURE & 24/7 EMERGENCY SERVICES
- Service Availability: 24-hour medical staff and healthcare support available within the campus for all students and staff.
- Hospital Bed Capacity: 30 beds.
- Clinical Team: 5 Doctors, 5 Nurses, Pharmacists, and Medical Assistants.
- Facilities Available:
  * In-house Pharmacy
  * Dispensary
  * Mini Operation Theatre (OT)
  * On-Campus 24/7 Ambulance Services: Provided by the Institute to attend to emergency cases and transport critical patients to secondary or tertiary hospitals in Nizamabad, Nirmal, or Hyderabad.
  * Housekeeping: Strict hygiene and daily hospital cleanliness maintained.
