RGUKT provides its students and staff with primary health facilities and basic medical support. A medical centre with the required facilities is available within the campus.

24 hours
in the campus. Housekeeping staff maintains strict cleanliness in the hospital. Ambulance services are provided by the Institute to attend to emergency cases and also when more critical cases need to be taken to secondary or tertiary medical centres.
