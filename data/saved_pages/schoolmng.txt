About the Department
Established 2010
The School of Management at RGUKT Basar offers management education as part of the integrated six-year B.Tech programme, with a minor in Management available to students across all engineering disciplines. The school equips students with managerial skills, entrepreneurial thinking, and leadership capabilities to complement their technical expertise.
Vision & Mission
Vision
To develop future leaders with a blend of technical expertise and managerial acumen, capable of driving innovation and sustainable growth.
Mission
To provide quality management education integrated with engineering studies, nurturing entrepreneurship, leadership, and decision-making skills essential for professional success in a competitive global environment.
