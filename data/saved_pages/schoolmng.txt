SCHOOL OF MANAGEMENT (MANAGEMENT STUDIES) — RGUKT BASAR

1. DEPARTMENT OVERVIEW & PROGRAMMES
- Established: 2010
- Total Faculty Members: 6
- Academic Scope: Offers management education integrated into the six-year B.Tech programme, equipping engineering students with managerial skills, entrepreneurial thinking, and leadership capabilities.
- Specialized Minor Programme: Offers an official "Minor in Management" as an elective programme available to students across all engineering disciplines.
- Academic Programmes: B.Tech (Management foundation & Minor in Management).

2. VISION & MISSION
- Vision:
  To develop future leaders with a blend of technical expertise and managerial acumen, capable of driving innovation and sustainable growth.
- Mission:
  To provide quality management education integrated with engineering studies, nurturing entrepreneurship, leadership, and decision-making skills essential for professional success in a competitive global environment.
