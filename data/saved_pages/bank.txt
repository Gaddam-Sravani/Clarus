BANK & ATM — RGUKT BASAR
=================================================================

BANK BRANCH ON CAMPUS
- Bank: State Bank of India (SBI)
- Purpose: Meets banking needs of students, faculty and staff. Handles account opening, fee payments, scholarships, and other banking services within campus premises.
- Location: Inside RGUKT Basar Campus
- Timings:
  - Mon–Fri: 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM
  - Sat: 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM

ATM
- Location: Near Main Gate, RGUKT Campus
- Availability: 24 × 7
- Network: SBI ATM — all networks accepted

SERVICES AVAILABLE
- Savings and current account opening for students and staff
- Scholarship and stipend credit directly to accounts
- Fee payment and demand draft facility
- NEFT / RTGS transfers
- Internet banking and mobile banking enrolment
- ATM / Debit card issuance
