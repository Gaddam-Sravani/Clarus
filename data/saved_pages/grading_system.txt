VII. GRADING SYSTEM AND AWARD OF DIVISION

1. ABSOLUTE GRADING SYSTEM FOR PUC PROGRAMME
Applicable for PUC batches of the 6-Year Integrated Course:

S.No | Performance    | Marks Range | Letter Grade | Grade Point per Credit
-----|----------------|-------------|--------------|-----------------------
1    | Excellent      | 90 – 100    | Ex           | 10
2    | Very good      | 80 – 89     | A            | 9
3    | Good           | 70 – 79     | B            | 8
4    | Fair           | 60 – 69     | C            | 7
5    | Average        | 50 – 59     | D            | 6
6    | Pass           | 40 – 49     | P            | 5
7    | Remedial/Fail  | < 40        | R            | 0

- Highest possible CGPA in the system is 10.00.

Pass Requirements for PUC:
- For students admitted from Academic Year 2016-17 onwards:
  * Theory: Minimum 40% marks in End Semester Examination (ESE) AND an overall minimum of 40% in ESE + Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) (P Grade).
  * Laboratory: Minimum 50% marks in End Semester Examination (ESE) AND an overall minimum of 50% in ESE + CIE (D Grade).
- For students admitted before Academic Year 2016-17:
  * Theory: Minimum 40% marks in ESE + CIE (P Grade).
  * Laboratory: Minimum 40% marks in ESE + CIE (P Grade).
- Failure Condition: If a student fails to secure minimum marks/grade in any subject, they must appear for supplementary/remedial examination only in the failed subject.

Moderation of PUC Results:
- Evaluated by a committee convened by Controller of Examinations (CoE):
  * Vice Chancellor / Director (Chairman)
  * Dean Academics (Member)
  * Head of the Department of concerned subject (Member)
  * Concerned subject faculty (Member)
  * Controller of Examinations (Convener)
- Moderation rules: Adopted if percentage of remedial students in that subject exceeds 10%.
  * If percentage of students scoring (A + Ex) >= 20%, no moderation is adopted.
  * If (A + Ex) < 20%, add 1 mark to all students and re-evaluate. Repeat adding 1 mark until (A + Ex) reaches >= 20% or up to a maximum of 5 marks added, whichever occurs earlier.


2. GRADING SYSTEM FOR B.TECH ENGINEERING STREAMS

I. Absolute Grading System (Admitted from Academic Year 2016-17 onwards):

S.No | Performance    | Marks Range | Letter Grade | Grade Point per Credit
-----|----------------|-------------|--------------|-----------------------
1    | Excellent      | 90 – 100    | Ex           | 10
2    | Very good      | 80 – 89     | A            | 9
3    | Good           | 70 – 79     | B            | 8
4    | Fair           | 60 – 69     | C            | 7
5    | Average        | 50 – 59     | D            | 6
6    | Pass           | 40 – 49     | P            | 5
7    | Remedial/Fail  | < 40        | R            | 0

- Highest possible CGPA in the system is 10.00.

Pass Requirements for B.Tech (from 2016-17 onwards):
- Theory: Minimum 40% marks in End Semester Examination (ESE) AND overall minimum 40% in ESE + CIE (P Grade).
- Laboratory: Minimum 50% marks in ESE AND overall minimum 50% in ESE + CIE (D Grade).
- Internship / Project / Comprehensive Viva / Seminar: Minimum 50% marks in overall evaluation (D Grade).

Moderation of B.Tech Results (from 2016-17 onwards):
- Convened by Controller of Examinations with VC/Director, Dean Academics, HOD, and Subject Faculty.
- Moderation adopted if percentage of remedial students exceeds 15%.
  * Add 1 mark iteratively until (A + Ex) >= 20% or a maximum of 5 marks are added.

II. Relative & Absolute Combined Grading System (Admitted before Academic Year 2016-17):
- Fixed cut-offs:
  * 90 – 100: Ex (10)
  * 80 – 89: A (9)
  * 70 – 79: B (8)
  * 60 – 69: C (7)
  * 50 – 59: D (6)
  * < 50: R (0)
- Pass requirements: Theory & Lab minimum 50% or cut-off for D grade, whichever is lower.
- Relative adjustment: Top 5% border mark sets Ex cut-off; next 20% border mark sets A cut-off. Lower of relative and absolute is adopted, limited to maximum deviation of +/- 10 marks from fixed cut-offs.


3. SGPA AND CGPA CALCULATION FORMULAS

• Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA):
  Formula: SGPA = Sum(Ci * GPi) / Sum(Ci)
  Where:
  * Ci = Credits registered for course i in that semester
  * GPi = Grade Point obtained in course i
  * Summation is over all courses taken in that semester, with 'n' subjects registered.

• Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA):
  Formula: CGPA = Sum(Ci * Si) / Sum(Ci)
  Where:
  * m = Total number of semesters under consideration
  * Ci = Total number of credits registered during semester i
  * Si = SGPA of semester i
  * SGPA and CGPA are rounded off to the second decimal place.

• Impact of Remedial ('R') Grade:
  * If a student gets an 'R' grade in any subject, SGPA and CGPA are tentatively calculated taking zero points (0) for that subject.
  * When the 'R' grade is substituted by a passing grade in a subsequent remedial examination, the SGPA and CGPA of all semesters from the earliest 'R' grade semester are recomputed and recorded.


4. AWARD OF DIVISION / CLASSIFICATION OF DEGREE

For students admitted from Academic Year 2016-17 onwards:
S.No | CGPA Range          | PUC Class Awarded | Engineering Class Awarded
-----|---------------------|-------------------|--------------------------
1    | CGPA >= 8.00        | Distinction       | Distinction
2    | 7.00 <= CGPA < 8.00 | First Class       | First Class
3    | 6.00 <= CGPA < 7.00 | Second Class      | Second Class
4    | 5.00 <= CGPA < 6.00 | Pass Class        | Pass Class
5    | CGPA < 5.00         | Remedial          | Remedial

For students admitted before Academic Year 2016-17:
S.No | CGPA Range          | PUC Class Awarded | Engineering Class Awarded
-----|---------------------|-------------------|--------------------------
1    | CGPA >= 8.00        | Distinction       | Distinction
2    | 7.00 <= CGPA < 8.00 | First Class       | First Class
3    | 6.00 <= CGPA < 7.00 | Second Class      | Second Class
4    | 5.00 <= CGPA < 6.00 | Pass Class        | Remedial
5    | CGPA < 5.00         | Remedial          | Remedial

Distinction Rule: Eligible for distinction only when CGPA >= 8.00 AND all subjects passed in a single attempt.

Percentage Conversion Formula:
Average Percentage of Marks = CGPA * 10


5. REVALUATION RULES AND PROCEDURES
- Application: Written plain-paper application submitted to the Director within 15 days of result declaration.
- Revaluation Fee: Demand draft / bank challan of Rs. 100/- per subject drawn in favor of Director, RGUKT-Basar.
- Mark Revision Criteria: The higher of original and revaluated marks is finalized ONLY IF the difference is >= 10% of original marks. If difference < 10%, original marks remain unchanged.


6. CONCESSION FOR PHYSICALLY HANDICAPPED (PH) STUDENTS
- Concession: 10% reduction over pass marks granted to PH students under Hearing Impaired, Orthopedically Handicapped, Visually Challenged, and Deaf/Dumb categories per government orders.
