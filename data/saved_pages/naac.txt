NAAC ACCREDITATION & IQAC CELL — RGUKT BASAR

1. NAAC ACCREDITATION STATUS & RECOGNITION
- Accrediting Body: National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC), Bangalore.
- Grade Awarded: 'C' Grade.
- Accreditation Cycle: 1st Cycle.
- Institutional Status: Recognized under Section 2(f) and Section 12B of the University Grants Commission (UGC) Act, 1956, as a State University (Technical).

2. INTERNAL QUALITY ASSURANCE CELL (IQAC)
- Established: 2017.
- Primary Objective: Responsible for developing quality benchmarks, parameters, and facilitating the internalization of a quality culture across all academic and administrative activities at RGUKT Basar.
- AQAR Reports: Annual Quality Assurance Reports prepared and submitted from 2017-18 through 2023-24 (available on request via IQAC).
  * AQAR 2023-24
  * AQAR 2022-23
  * AQAR 2021-22
  * AQAR 2020-21
  * AQAR 2019-20
  * AQAR 2018-19
  * AQAR 2017-18
- Documentation: Self Study Report (SSR) and Data Validation & Verification (DVV) clarifications maintained by IQAC.
