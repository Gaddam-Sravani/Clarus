CIRCULAR

Subject: Academic Section - Amendment in University Academic Rules and Regulations - Alteration of Point X(1)(3)(e) regarding completion of Pre-University Course B19 Batch onwards - Reg.

Ref: Approval of Minutes of XXVII Executive Committee Meeting held on 13.01.2021.

*****

This is to inform that several students who fail to meet the promotion criteria (as mentioned in X(1)(3)(d)) to the B.Tech 1st year, but has satisfied the PUC pass requirements shall leave the University with PUC Completion Certificate.

In this regard, the Executive committee has approved the proposal of amendment of Point No. X(1)(3)(e) in Academic Rules & Regulations of the University for PUC-1 & PUC-II. The following is the alteration approved:

XXVII. COMPLETION OF PUC COURSE:-
"EC has reviewed the PUC program and resolved to permit PUC students with backlogs to write end semester examinations along with regular students beyond three years without providing accommodation and food. EC has accorded to implement from B19 batch students onwards and be given PUC completion certificate upon clearing all backlog subjects. All such students lose their eligibility to Engineering admission at RGUKT.

EC also has resolved to give no exemption on completing 2 years PUC program in 3 years to be eligible for the engineering program at RGUKT".
