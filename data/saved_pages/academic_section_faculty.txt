DSW: Rules relating to issue of Outpass-Action for fake reasons.


As per the directions of Director, it is to inform all the students of RGUKT that No outpass will be issued to the students as Saturday happens to be a working day. All the students are to make a note of this and cooperate with the Administration.

Students approaching for outpass without having valid reason is treated as an offense and an in-disciple action will be initiated against such students. Any student insisting to consider their case under emergency and medical grounds without substantial evidence will be treated very seriously and resulting in adverse remarks in their records. Such entries will influence their academics, awards and placement opportunities.
Academic Section: Circular - Rules during Academic Hours.

Dear Students,

It is to inform all the students that they need to abide by the following rules during academic hours.

1.	Students of P1 to E3 should be inside the classroom by 9:00 am to 1:00 pm in the Morning session and 2:00 pm to 5:00 pm 
        in the afternoon session on all the working days.
2.	Students of E4 should maintain their time schedule as per the time table of the open Elective Classes.
3.	Students should wear a uniform and university ID card.
4.	Maintaining discipline with fellow students.
5.     Cell phones and any electronic gadgets are prohibited with immediate effect.
6.     Damaging University property is an offense. if found, will be held liable for the penalty.
        
Students needs to adhere to the above points otherwise university will take necessary action.

		 Sd/-
Associate Dean of Engineering
Associate Dean of Sciences & Humanities
