2. OFFICIAL CIRCULAR: RULES DURING ACADEMIC HOURS
(Issued by: Associate Dean of Engineering & Associate Dean of Sciences & Humanities)
1. Classroom Timings:
   - Students of P1 to E3 must be inside their classrooms from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM in the Morning session, and from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM in the Afternoon session on all working days. Every student must be inside the classroom by 9:00 AM.
   - Students of E4 must maintain their time schedule as per their Open Elective classes timetable.
2. Uniform & Identification: Mandatory to wear university uniform and display university ID card.
3. Conduct: Maintain discipline with fellow students.
4. Mobile Phone & Gadget Ban: Mobile phones and any electronic gadgets are strictly prohibited during academic hours with immediate effect.
5. Property Damage: Damaging university property is an offense; students found responsible are held liable for financial penalties and disciplinary action.

3. OFFICIAL CIRCULAR: DSW RULES RELATING TO OUTPASS & ACTION FOR FAKE REASONS
(Issued as per directions of the Director, RGUKT Basar)
1. Saturday Restriction: No outpass will be issued to students on Saturdays, as Saturday is a regular working day.
2. Fake Emergency/Medical Claims: Approaching for an outpass without a valid reason is treated as an offense. Any student insisting on emergency or medical outpass without substantial evidence will be treated with serious disciplinary action, resulting in adverse remarks entered into their permanent official records.
3. Impact: Adverse remarks will directly influence their academic evaluations, university awards, and campus placement opportunities.
