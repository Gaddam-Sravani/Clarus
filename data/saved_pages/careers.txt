RGUKT BASAR — CAREERS & RECRUITMENT
================================================================================

OVERVIEW & STATUTORY NORMS
Rajiv Gandhi University of Knowledge Technologies (RGUKT) Basar invites applications for faculty and non-teaching positions periodically. All recruitment processes are conducted in strict compliance with RGUKT statutes and Government of Telangana regulations. Official advertisements are published in leading newspapers and posted on the official portal under "Current Openings" and "Notices & Downloads".

TYPES OF POSITIONS RECRUITED

1. Faculty Positions:
   - Professor
   - Associate Professor
   - Assistant Professor
   - Lecturer / Instructor

2. Non-Teaching Positions:
   - Administrative Staff
   - Technical / Laboratory Staff
   - Library Staff
   - Accounts & Finance Staff

APPLICATION PROCEDURE
1. Application Form: Download the official prescribed application form from the respective notification link on the university portal.
2. Documentation: Fill in all required information and attach self-attested photocopies of educational certificates, experience credentials, and caste/category documents.
3. Submission: Submit the completed application form to the designated address specified in the notification before the stipulated deadline.
4. Selection: Only shortlisted candidates are summoned for the interview or written test.
