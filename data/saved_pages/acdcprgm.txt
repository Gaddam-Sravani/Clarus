ACADEMIC PROGRAMMES & DEGREE STRUCTURE — RGUKT BASAR

1. UNDERGRADUATE 6-YEAR INTEGRATED B.TECH PROGRAMME
RGUKT offers a unique 6-year integrated course after 10th class (SSC) leading to a Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) degree. The program is divided into two parts:
- Part 1: Two-year Pre-University Course (PUC), equivalent to Telangana Intermediate (covering Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and Life Sciences — equivalent to MPC + BiPC), alongside broad-based education in humanities and liberal arts.
- Part 2: Four-year Engineering Course across 8 semesters.

2. B.TECH DEGREE DISCIPLINES OFFERED (7 BRANCHES)
Upon successful completion of the 6 years of education (each year consisting of 2 semesters) and prescribed credit requirements, graduates are awarded a B.Tech degree in one of the following 7 engineering disciplines:
1. Chemical Engineering
2. Civil Engineering
3. Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
4. Electrical Engineering (EEE)
5. Electronics & Communications Engineering (ECE)
6. Materials Science and Metallurgical Engineering (MME)
7. Mechanical Engineering (ME)

3. POSTGRADUATE PROGRAMMES (M.TECH)
RGUKT Basar offers Master of Technology (M.Tech) degrees designed for teaching and research careers in computational engineering and sciences in the following specializations:
1. Computer Science & Engineering
2. Electronics & Communication Engineering
3. Computational Mechanics in Mechanical Engineering

4. TUITION FEES & FINANCIAL STRUCTURE
- Subsidized Education: As a government-sponsored university, tuition fees at RGUKT are highly subsidized by the Telangana Government.
- Annual Fee for Pre-University Course (PUC): Rs. 36,000/- per year for Telangana State (TS) students for the first two years.
- Inclusions: The annual Rs. 36,000/- fee covers tuition, lodging (hostel accommodation), and boarding (mess dining).
