DEPARTMENT OF BIO SCIENCE (BIO SCIENCES) — RGUKT BASAR
=================================================================

OVERVIEW & ESTABLISHMENT
- Established: 2010
- Faculty Members: 8
- Academic Focus: Interdisciplinary education encompassing Bioinformatics, Biomedical Engineering, Biomaterial sciences, biodiversity, ecology, environment, and sustainability.
- Objective: Builds need-based, economical, and sustainable technologies for grassroots development of rural lifestyle and economy, addressing critical issues in rural Telangana particularly in agriculture and health sectors.

VISION & MISSION
- Vision:
  The main vision of the department is to build need-based economical and sustainable technologies for grassroots development of rural lifestyle and economy. To address the critical issues of rural Telangana, mainly in agriculture and health sector. To impart education on interdisciplinary domains (viz. Bioinformatics, Biomedical & Biomaterial etc.) and fundamental aspects of biodiversity, ecology, environment and sustainability issues — key to the nation's economical and social prosperity.
- Mission:
  No other science has been as pervading and amalgamating as biological sciences. Integration with other disciplines is essential for its manifestation, and the molecular understanding of living processes has been inspiring for innovative applications in engineering.

MAIN THRUST AREAS OF RESEARCH
1. Bioinformatics: Developing novel algorithms for biological analysis and deriving biological implications from sequence analysis and structural predictions.
2. Agriculture: Development of biofertilizers, nutraceuticals (medicinal foods), and harnessing sustainable energy and useful industrial products from agricultural and food waste.
3. Environment Remediation: Development of economical and efficient water purification technologies with the integration of nanoscience and screening of plant-based materials as molecular sieves.
4. Sustainable Technologies: Utilising biological resources to recycle products, derive energy from waste, build resilient materials from biological sources, and develop eco-friendly products.
5. Biophysical Chemistry of Proteins: Investigating structural and functional attributes of novel proteins using biophysical, molecular biology, and biochemical techniques.
6. Drug Design and Development: In silico rational drug design and development of nano biocompatible materials, validated through in vitro and in vivo approaches.
