XIII. TRANSITORY REGULATIONS — RGUKT BASAR

1. CURRICULUM & SYLLABUS CHANGE POLICY
- Whenever a course or scheme of instruction is changed in a particular semester or academic year, two (2) more examinations following thereafter shall be conducted according to the old syllabus, regulations, and examination pattern.
- Candidates who do not appear at these two examinations or fail in them shall take the examination subsequently according to the changed (new) syllabus, regulations, and examination pattern.
