This circular, dated March 22, 2021, from Rajiv Gandhi University of Knowledge Technologies (Basar), communicates Rule XIV (Rules Regarding Conduct and Discipline) of the Academic Rules and Regulations. It was issued to address students roaming in non-academic areas during instructional hours amidst ongoing COVID-19 concerns.

Key areas covered in the document include:

    General Conduct: Expectations for students to show courtesy and respect toward faculty, university employees, hostel wardens, visitors, and fellow students—especially incoming first-year students.

    Prohibited Acts: A list of twelve actions considered gross violations of the code of conduct, including ragging, falsifying official forms, damaging library books or university property, IT/server misuse, exam malpractice, leaving campus or organizing group events without permission, substance abuse, and disrupting the academic environment.

    Disciplinary Measures & Authorities: Outlines potential punishments ranging from fines and reprimands to hostel expulsion, exam debarment, rustication, or university expulsion. It also specifies the authorities responsible for handling offenses based on where they occur.

    Disciplinary Committees: Details the composition and procedures for the Standing Disciplinary Committee (for major indiscipline) and the Committee on Prevention of Examination Malpractices (for exam-related offenses), both of which forward their recommendations to the Vice-Chancellor.
