OFFICIAL NOTICE: VISITOR RULES & CAMPUS ENTRY REGULATIONS — RGUKT BASAR
Authority: Office of the Dean, Student Welfare (DSW), RGUKT Basar. Notice Date: 18-04-2015.

1. RULES FOR VISITORS & PARENTS
- Permitted Visiting Day: Parents are allowed to meet their children ONLY on Sundays.
- Visiting Hours: Visitor timings are strictly from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM only. Campus security will not permit visitors outside of these hours.
- Mandatory Identification: No parents or relatives are permitted inside campus without producing an Identity Card.
- Relatives & Friends: Relatives and friends of students are strictly not allowed to enter the campus unless they are physically accompanied by the student's parents.
- No Hospitality Provided: No visitors will be provided food or accommodation on campus under any circumstances.
- Unauthorized Outsiders: Unauthorized outsiders found living or wandering on campus will be fined to the maximum extent and handed over to local police authorities.

2. CAMPUS ENTRY & STAY RULES FOR STUDENTS
- Passed-Out / Unregistered Students: No passed-out students or unregistered students are permitted to stay on campus or in hostels.
- Remedial & Backlog Students:
  * Remedial / backlog students are strictly NOT allowed to stay on campus before or after the day of examination.
  * Permitted on campus ONLY on the day of examination upon producing valid examination registration proof / hall ticket.
- Summer Courses: During summer courses, only registered students are permitted on campus upon producing registration proof.
- Penalty: Any student found living unauthorized on campus or harboring unauthorized individuals will be imposed with the maximum financial penalty and subjected to disciplinary action.
