OVERVIEW & METRICS
- Established: 2010
- Academic Programmes: Four-year undergraduate B.Tech program
- Faculty Members: 11 (department header indicates 11; overview mentions nine faculty members conducting cutting-edge research)
- Laboratories: 5
- Curriculum & Training:
  - Foundational coursework combined with specialized electives in emerging fields:
    - Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)
    - Nanotechnology
    - Petroleum Engineering
  - Practical Activities: Industrial trips, mandatory summer internships, and annual live projects.

VISION & MISSION
- Vision:
  To become the most sought-after center of excellence, engaged in training and shaping students as professionals for higher education and process industries, both in India and abroad.
- Mission:
  Imparting contemporary technical education & training manpower to create skilled human resource talent pool to serve and manage the process industries globally with a sense of responsibility towards society and environment.
