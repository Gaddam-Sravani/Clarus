CONFERENCE & SEMINAR HALLS — RGUKT BASAR
=================================================================

OVERVIEW
RGUKT Basar has well-equipped conference halls and seminar rooms that support academic events, workshops, faculty meetings, and administrative gatherings. The halls are equipped with modern audio-visual infrastructure for effective presentations and video conferencing.

VENUE SPECIFICATIONS & CAPACITIES

1. Seminar Hall
   - Capacity: ~300 seats
   - Facilities & Audio-Visual:
     - LCD Projector & Screen
     - PA System & Podium
     - Air-conditioned

2. Conference Room
   - Capacity: ~50 seats
   - Facilities:
     - Video Conferencing Setup
     - High-speed Wi-Fi
     - Air-conditioned

3. Auditorium
   - Capacity: ~1000 seats
   - Facilities & Uses:
     - Stage & Sound System
     - Large LED Screen
     - Used for convocations & cultural events

BOOKING & USAGE PROCEDURE
