XIV. RULES REGARDING CONDUCT AND DISCIPLINE — RGUKT BASAR

1. GENERAL CODE OF CONDUCT & COURTESY
- Diligence & Respect: Students shall show due diligence and courtesy to teachers, Wardens, Sports Officers, NCC Officers, university employees, and campus visitors.
- Student Relations: Friendly and considerate relationships must be maintained with fellow students, especially junior/newly admitted students. Acts of ragging are considered gross indiscipline and severely dealt with.

2. GROSS VIOLATIONS OF CODE OF CONDUCT (12 OFFENSES)
The following acts constitute gross violation of discipline and invoke severe punitive action:
i. Ragging in any form.
ii. Furnishing false statements of any kind while filling official forms, scholarship applications, etc.
iii. Mutilation, theft, or unauthorized possession of library books.
iv. Misuse of internet/email facilities, tampering, or hacking with university/hostel servers.
v. Adoption of unfair means in examinations (cheating/malpractice).
vi. Organizing or participating in unauthorized group activities inside or outside campus without prior written permission of the Director.
vii. Leaving campus without intimating the hostel warden and without an approved outpass.
viii. Displaying lack of decorum, indecent behavior, or discourtesy anywhere inside or outside campus.
ix. Willfully damaging or stealing property belonging to the University, hostel, or fellow students.
x. Possession, consumption, or distribution of alcoholic drinks, smoking, or hallucinogenic drugs/narcotics.
xi. Creating unrest, noisy/unseemly behavior, disturbing studies, or disrupting the academic environment.
xii. Inviting public personalities or organizing public events without prior permission of competent authorities.

Penalties: Commensurate with offense gravity, punishments include official reprimand, financial fines, expulsion from hostels, debarment from examinations, rustication for a specified period, or permanent expulsion from the University.

3. DISCIPLINARY JURISDICTION
- Minor / Immediate Offenses:
  * In Hostels: The Warden has authority to reprimand or impose fines.
  * In Classrooms / Departments: The Head of Department (HOD) has authority.
  * Across University / Elsewhere: The Director has authority.
- All punishments beyond an oral reprimand must be reported to the Chairman of the Standing Disciplinary Committee.

4. STANDING DISCIPLINARY COMMITTEE (FOR MAJOR INDISCIPLINE)
Appointed by the Vice-Chancellor to investigate major acts of indiscipline:
1. Director / Administrative Officer (Chairperson)
2. Dean of Academic Affairs (Member)
3. Warden of the student's concerned hostel (Member)
4. Student Representative nominated by the Vice-Chancellor (Member)
5. Student Representative nominated by the Director (Member)
6. Chief Warden (Member)
7. Head of Department (HOD) of the student's department (Member)
8. Dean of Student Welfare (Convener)
- Recommendations and suggested punishments are forwarded to the Vice-Chancellor for final execution.

5. COMMITTEE ON PREVENTION OF EXAMINATION MALPRACTICES
Handles cases of unfair means during university examinations:
1. Director / Administrative Officer (Chairperson)
2. Controller of Examinations (Member)
3. Head of the Department concerned (Member)
4. Invigilator reporting the malpractice case (Member)
5. Chief Superintendent of Examinations (Member)
6. Dean of Academic Affairs (Convener)
- Recommendations on disciplinary penalties are forwarded to the Vice-Chancellor for final orders.
