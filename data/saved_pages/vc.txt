Profile
Prof. A. Govardhan is a Senior Professor of Computer Science & Engineering with 30 years of teaching and research experience. He has served in and held several academic and administrative positions including Rector, JNTU Hyderabad; Registrar I/c, JNTUH; Principal (JNTUH CEH, JNTUH CEJ); Director, School of Information Technology, JNTUH; Director of Evaluation; Nodal Officer (JNTUH CER); Head, Dept. of CSE, JNTUH CEH; and Chairman & Member on Boards of Studies of various State & Central Universities.
He did his 10th standard from Z.P.H.S. Choutuppal, B.E. (CSE) from Osmania University, M.Tech from JNU New Delhi, Ph.D from JNTU Hyderabad, and Post Graduate Diploma in Leadership from EMERITUS Singapore (a collaborative programme of Tuck Executive Education at Dartmouth, Columbia Business School Executive Education and MIT Management Executive Education).
