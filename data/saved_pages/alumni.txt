RGUKT BASAR — ALUMNI AFFAIRS & INTERNATIONAL RELATIONS
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
The Alumni Affairs & International Relations Office fosters and sustains enduring relationships between RGUKT Basar and its global alumni fraternity across diverse engineering domains and industries.

VISION
"Form a powerful network that benefits the University and its community for a lifetime."

OBJECTIVES
1. Foster and bridge the interaction between the University and its Alumni.
2. Encourage alumni to contribute towards the all-round development of current students.
3. Improve relationships among Alumni members to bring about harmony and brotherhood.
4. Keep all alumni informed and involved to achieve a cohesive global alumni network.
5. Support alumni involvement and advisory participation in university affairs.

OFFICIAL PORTALS & WEBSITES
- Official RGUKT Alumni Portal: www.alumni.rgukt.ac.in
- Alumni Association Website: www.rgubaa.in
- Official Email: alumni@rgukt.ac.in

ALUMNI PORTAL FEATURES
- Alumni Directory: Comprehensive searchable directory to connect with alumni by batch, city, domain, or company.
- Mentorship: Dedicated module for alumni to mentor and guide current undergraduate students.
- Career Opportunities: Platform to share and discover job openings and internships within the network.
- Events & Meets: Updates on alumni meets, regional chapter reunions, and campus activities.
