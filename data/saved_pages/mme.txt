RGUKT BASAR — DEPARTMENT OF METALLURGICAL AND MATERIALS ENGINEERING (MME)
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
The Department of Metallurgical and Materials Engineering (MME) provides rigorous academic instruction and cutting-edge experimental research in metallurgy, materials synthesis, advanced processing, characterization, and failure analysis.

ACADEMIC PROGRAMMES OFFERED
- B.Tech in Metallurgical and Materials Engineering
- Total Faculty Members: 12

ADVANCED RESEARCH INFRASTRUCTURE & LABORATORIES
The department is equipped with advanced analytical instrumentation and materials synthesis facilities, including:
1. X-Ray Diffraction (XRD) system
2. Field Emission Scanning Electron Microscope (FE-SEM)
3. Friction Stir Welding (FSW) research facility

VISION & MISSION
- Vision:
  "The department of Metallurgical and Materials Engineering provides the rural students with quality education, developing the fundamental understanding along with ethics and technological innovations to serve the needs of the society."
- Mission:
  "To showcase the broad spectrum of Metallurgical and Materials Engineering. To promote a collaborative bridge between academic institutions, research organizations and industries to address the current and future societal challenges. Develop technology and prepare students in the relevant areas of metallurgical and materials engineering."
