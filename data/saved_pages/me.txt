RGUKT BASAR — DEPARTMENT OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING
================================================================================

OVERVIEW & ESTABLISHMENT
- Established: 2010
- Annual Intake: 120+ B.Tech students and 18 M.Tech students
- Total Faculty Members: 27
- Academic Programmes Offered:
  1. B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering
  2. M.Tech in Computational Mechanics (commenced in 2016)

LABORATORIES & COMPUTING INFRASTRUCTURE
- Total Laboratories: 14 laboratories equipped with advanced manufacturing and testing machinery.
- Advanced Equipment: Wire Electrical Discharge Machining (Wire EDM), 3D Printers, Coordinate Measuring Machine (CMM), and CNC Systems.
- Design & Simulation Laboratory: Equipped with 90 high-performance computer systems supporting CAD, CATIA, SOLIDWORKS, ANSYS, and MATLAB software suites.
- Core Undergraduate Laboratories:
  1. Applied Thermodynamics Lab
  2. Automation & Robotics Lab
  3. CAD / CAM Lab
  4. Fuels & Lubricants Lab
  5. Instrumentation & Control Lab
  6. Materials Engineering Lab
  7. Metrology Lab
  8. Strength of Materials Lab
- Institutional & Industrial Partnerships: Central Institute of Tool Design (CITD - Hyderabad), Non-Ferrous Materials Technology Development Centre (NFTDC), and NASSCOM.

VISION & MISSION
- Vision:
  "To be a global knowledge hub in mechanical engineering education, research, entrepreneurship and industry outreach services. To develop and serve practicing engineers, industry, government, and technical societies through consultancy work."
- Mission:
  "Impart quality education and training to nurture globally competitive mechanical engineers. Provide state-of-the-art research facilities to create, interpret, apply and disseminate knowledge. Develop linkages with world-class educational institutions and R&D organizations for excellence in teaching, research, and consultancy services."
