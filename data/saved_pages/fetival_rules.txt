OFFICE OF THE DEAN OF STUDENTS' WELFARE — OFFICIAL NOTICE
================================================================================

NOTICE DATE: 21-03-2016
AUTHORITY: Issued by the Dean (Students' Welfare) as per the instructions of the Hon'ble Vice Chancellor.

PURPOSE
Instructions issued to ensure a peaceful, clean, and green celebration of the Holi festival on campus, preventing disturbances and damage to university property.

OFFICIAL INSTRUCTIONS & CODE OF CONDUCT
1. Gender Segregation: Boys and girls are not permitted to celebrate together in any location across the campus; celebrations must be conducted separately.
2. Designated Celebration Venues:
   - Boys: Permitted to celebrate only in Sathavaahana grounds.
   - Girls: Permitted to celebrate only within their respective hostel premises (i.e., in the cellar or open spaces of the girl's hostels).
3. Prohibited Areas: Playing Holi is strictly forbidden in Academic Blocks, classrooms, hostel living rooms, and the Campus Hospital.
4. Consent: Forcible application of colors or compelling any student to participate is strictly prohibited.
5. Permitted Colors & Substances:
   - Eggs are strictly prohibited.
   - Due to the hazards of chemical colors, only natural/eco-friendly colors are allowed.
6. Security Compliance: University security personnel are instructed to strictly enforce these regulations and maintain continuous vigilance.
7. Disciplinary Penalties: Any violation will be viewed seriously with stringent disciplinary action, and the cost of any property damages will be recovered directly from defaulters.

SPECIAL FESTIVAL DAY MESS & DINING ARRANGEMENTS (ONE-DAY ARRANGEMENT)
On the day of the Holi festival, dining venues are segregated as follows:
- Girls (All Batches): S.S. Mess only.
- Boys (P1 to E1 Batches): Marvel Mess only.
- Boys (E2 to E4 Batches): Telangana Mess only.

FESTIVAL DAY MEAL TIMINGS
- Breakfast: 6:30 AM to 8:00 AM
- Lunch: 1:00 PM to 2:30 PM (01.00 PM to 02.30 PM)
- Snacks: 5:00 PM to 5:45 PM
- Dinner: 7:30 PM to 9:00 PM
