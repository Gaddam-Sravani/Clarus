Gymnasium at RGUKT Basar
RGUKT Basar provides a fully equipped gymnasium for students, faculty, and staff to maintain physical fitness. The gym is open during designated hours and is supervised by a qualified physical trainer.
Timings
Morning: 5:30 AM – 8:00 AM
Evening: 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM
Open all days (except holidays)
Equipment
Treadmill & Elliptical
Free Weights & Dumbbells
Multi-gym Station
Cycling Machine
Guidelines
Entry for RGUKT students, faculty, and staff only — valid ID card required
Proper sports attire and footwear must be worn at all times
Users are requested to maintain hygiene and cleanliness
Machines must be wiped after use
No food or beverages inside the gym
Follow instructions of the gym supervisor at all times
