RGUKT BASAR — CAMPUS GYMNASIUM & FITNESS FACILITY
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
RGUKT Basar maintains a fully equipped campus gymnasium to promote physical fitness, wellness, and athletic conditioning for students, faculty, and staff. The gym is supervised by a qualified physical trainer during all operating hours.

OPERATING TIMINGS
- Morning Session: 5:30 AM – 8:00 AM
- Evening Session: 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM
- Operational Days: Open all days (closed on university holidays)

FITNESS EQUIPMENT AVAILABLE
1. Treadmills & Ellipticals (Cardiovascular fitness)
2. Free Weights & Dumbbells (Strength and resistance training)
3. Multi-Gym Station (Compound full-body workouts)
4. Stationary Cycling Machines

GYMNASIUM RULES & USER GUIDELINES
1. Eligibility & Entry: Open exclusively to RGUKT students, faculty members, and staff. A valid university ID card is strictly mandatory for admission.
2. Attire: Proper sports attire and clean athletic footwear/shoes must be worn at all times.
3. Cleanliness & Hygiene: Users must maintain cleanliness and hygiene. Workout machines and contact surfaces must be wiped down after use.
4. Food & Drinks: Consuming food items and beverages inside the gym premises is strictly prohibited.
5. Supervision: Users must strictly comply with all safety instructions and directives issued by the gym supervisor.
