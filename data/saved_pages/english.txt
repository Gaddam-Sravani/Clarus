RGUKT BASAR — DEPARTMENT OF ENGLISH
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
The Department of English provides foundational and advanced training in English language, professional communication, linguistics, phonetics, and literature across PUC and B.Tech engineering curricula. It manages language laboratory sessions and soft-skills training essential for campus placements and competitive examinations.
